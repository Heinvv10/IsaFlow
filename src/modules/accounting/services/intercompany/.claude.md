# intercompany/

Intercompany mirror posting (plan: `docs/plans/2026-11-17-intercompany-mirror-posting.md`). P1 = group connections (migrations 1000, 1002). **P2 (migration 1013) = the invoice + credit-note mirror**: an approved customer invoice / customer credit note leaves a message in the SENDER's outbox, the RECEIVER accepts it in the Bills > Intercompany inbox (a DRAFT supplier invoice / supplier credit note), and the auto-accept worker does the same as the receiver for a group side that asked for it. Tables: `intercompany_connections`, `intercompany_connection_sides`, `company_connection_keys`, `intercompany_messages`, `intercompany_document_links` (keys and the payment / allocation kinds wait for P4 / P6).

## Modules
- `connectionService.ts` — barrel plus `createGroupConnection`, `revokeConnection`, `connectionView`. `connectionSettingsService.ts` — `updateConnectionSettings` (the pair's agreement). `connectionSideService.ts` — `updateOwnSide`. `connectionRead.ts` — `listConnections`, `listGroupCandidates`. `connectionOptions.ts` — the side form's pick-lists.
- `connectionValidation.ts` — pure: request shapes (`parseCreateInput`, `parseSideInput`, `parsePairInput`), `autoAcceptRefusal`, `orderedPair`, `computeChecklist`. `connectionTypes.ts` — types and `ConnectionRefusal` (status 400/403/404/409). `connectionRoute.ts` — the routes' answer for a refusal.

## Caller contract
Called by `pages/api/accounting/intercompany/*` inside `withAuth`: `sql` is the request's transaction as app_user with the session company's GUCs, so RLS limits every read and write to the session company. Service functions take the session `companyId` (and `userId` where a role check needs it). A refusal is thrown as `ConnectionRefusal` before anything is written; a refused write inside a savepoint (`withSavepoint`) does not poison the request's transaction.

## Main function signatures
- `listConnections(companyId, connectionId?)` — this company's connections, each with the pair `settings`, `canEditSettings` (write role in both companies), ITS OWN side and setup checklist only.
- `createGroupConnection(userId, companyId, partnerCompanyId)` — write role in both; both current members of one group (asked of `intercompany_in_one_group`, the definer helper); a revoked group connection is re-activated by one `UPDATE ... WHERE status = 'revoked'`, never duplicated; a concurrent create is a 409.
- `updateConnectionSettings(userId, companyId, connectionId, input)` — mode / cut-over / interest, write role in BOTH companies, not once revoked.
- `updateOwnSide(companyId, connectionId, input)` — partial update (absent key unchanged, null clears) of the session company's side; upsert on (connection, company).
- `revokeConnection(companyId, connectionId)` — either party.

## Business rules
- The pair is stored ordered (`company_low_id < company_high_id`, `orderedPair`); at most one LIVE (not revoked) row per pair (partial unique index). Kind never changes. External connections cannot be created until P6 (the guard refuses the INSERT).
- Settlement mode, cut-over date and interest rate belong to the PAIR (the connection row), not to a side. A group connection's change needs a write role in both companies (service and `intercompany_connections_guard`).
- A company never reads or writes its partner's side: RLS is `company_id = session company`. Customer/supplier must be the company's own (non-deleted) and linked to one connection per company (partial unique indexes: a second link is a 409); loan and default expense accounts its own, active and non-header (`isHeaderSql`). The database re-checks ownership in `intercompany_sides_guard`, and refuses any change once the connection is revoked.
- `auto_accept` only on a `group` connection, and only while both companies are in one group: `autoAcceptRefusal` in the service, `intercompany_sides_guard` (ENABLE ALWAYS trigger) in the database.
- A revoked connection releases its sides' customer and supplier links (`intercompany_release_links`, a registered definer fired by the status change, including the group-leave revocation); re-activating does not restore them.
- Group membership holds for the life of a group connection, and the group must be ACTIVE (`company_groups.is_active`, which `deleteGroup` clears). `intercompany_in_one_group(a, b)` (SECURITY DEFINER, registered in `app_definer_reviews`) is checked on create, re-activate, message insert and auto-accept. It answers only for a signed-in user of one of the two companies (never the session's company GUC), so it is false when `auth.uid()` is NULL and the P2 auto-accept worker needs its own definer path; it is no membership oracle. A pair that does share a group takes the two companies' rows of `intercompany_company_locks` (closed to every app role, one row per company, made by a trigger on `companies`) FOR SHARE until commit; a join takes ALL the group's members' rows FOR SHARE; the changers are STATEMENT-level triggers with transition tables on `company_group_members` and `company_groups` (UPDATE and DELETE) that take their whole set (the leavers, the parties of the connections the change would end, the members connected to a leaver) FOR UPDATE NOWAIT all or nothing, retrying, so a waiting changer holds nothing and a multi-row statement locks one set; the wait is bounded (`app.intercompany_changer_budget_ms`, default 10 s) and then fails with SQLSTATE 55P03, which withErrorHandler answers 503 + Retry-After from any route. They never return before locking except for a change that changes nothing. The protocol needs READ COMMITTED: `intercompany_in_one_group`, every changer, the joiner and the sides guard refuse with 40001 at REPEATABLE READ / SERIALIZABLE (as migration 886 does for `company_users`). A leave, a bulk leave, a deactivation or a group deletion at the same moment waits and then revokes what was created, two of them serialise, and a stranger can hold nothing (lock protocol in the 1002 header). app_user has no table-level UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE on the group tables (it could `LOCK TABLE` them): UPDATE is granted by column, to what `groupCrudService` writes; a new writer of another column needs a column grant in a migration. They revoke the active group connections whose companies no longer share a current active group (`revoked_by` = `system:group-membership`, accepted by the connections guard only from migration_admin with a transaction-local flag) and switch their sides' `auto_accept` off. Whatever writes on a connection later (the P2 auto-accept worker) re-checks through the same helper at action time, not on its own read of `company_group_members`.
- Viewers read only: every app_user write on the five tables needs `intercompany_user_can_write(<acting company>)` in the guard (status changes, sides, sending and handling a message, links, keys).
- Messages: UNIQUE (from company, source doc type, source doc id, kind); for the allocation and payment kinds the source doc is the allocation / payment ROW id; inserted only on an ACTIVE connection between its two companies; the sender only inserts, the receiver moves a pending message on (a dismissal can be restored, accepted and rejected are final, accepting, rejecting and restoring need the connection active; an invoice / credit_note / interest_invoice names a source document of the SENDING company that fits its kind, and an accepted one names a target of the RECEIVING company that fits it: supplier invoice, or supplier credit note for a credit note; the kinds that create no document carry no target; a receiver document is the target of ONE accepted message per RECEIVING COMPANY, whatever the connection; a side's first save, a message send and a link insert read their connection FOR SHARE); `handled_via` is `auto` only when the session sets `app.intercompany_handled_via = 'auto'` AND the receiver's side has auto-accept on an active group connection; `error` holds a generic sentence, never raw SQL or exception text.
- Links (`intercompany_document_links`) are the authoritative pair record: a mirror link is made by the receiver of an accepted message and joins exactly its source and target documents (active at once; both are re-checked against the message's two companies, so a forged message row cannot link a third company's document); a manual pairing starts `pending_confirmation`, names a document that exists in the inserter's own company, and goes active when the other party confirms (a user with a write role in both companies of a group may confirm both); either party may unlink. A company document is in one live link at a time whatever the connection and the position (`intercompany_document_slots`, kept by a definer trigger: an active link holds both documents, a pending pairing only the side its inserter confirmed, so a partner cannot squat a document by proposing it; 1002 refuses to apply over duplicates; active links and their slots survive a revoke until a party unlinks); confirming needs the connection active and the confirmed side's document to exist in its company; one mirror link per message.

## P2: the mirror (messages in, drafts out)
Files: `mirrorPayload.ts` (PURE: build the sender's cents-exact snapshot, `allocateLineVat`, `parseMirrorPayload` = the receiver's untrusted-input check, `payloadBlocker` = ZAR only), `outboxRead.ts` + `outboxService.ts` (the sender: `mirrorApprovedInvoice`, `mirrorApprovedCreditNote`), `mirrorHooks.ts` (`withdrawForCancelledDocument`, `syncGroupRowFor`), `inboxRead.ts` / `inboxTypes.ts` / `inboxValidation.ts` (the receiver's list, counts, request shapes), `acceptContext.ts` (this company's side, account suggestions, duplicate lookup), `acceptBlockers.ts` (PURE), `acceptDocuments.ts` (the draft + the PDF copy), `acceptService.ts` (`previewAccept`, `acceptMessage`), `handleService.ts` (reject / dismiss / restore / `markMessageFailed`), `linkOrder.ts` (PURE), `receiverTx.ts` + `autoAcceptWorker.ts` (the worker), `mirrorErrors.ts`.

### Caller contract
- `approveCustomerInvoice` calls `mirrorApprovedInvoice(companyId, invoiceId)` after the PDF seal; `approveCreditNote` calls `mirrorApprovedCreditNote` for a CUSTOMER note and `syncGroupRowFor` for a SUPPLIER one. `cancelCustomerInvoice` / `cancelCreditNote` (customer) call `withdrawForCancelledDocument`; `approveSupplierInvoice` / `cancelSupplierInvoice` / `cancelCreditNote` (supplier) call `syncGroupRowFor`. All run on the caller's transaction inside a savepoint and NEVER throw: a failure is logged AT ERROR (SQLSTATE only) and the document change stands.
- **Never-mirrored cases, and the re-send.** A mirror that fails (the database refuses, a session without a write role, a revoked connection), an approval that happened before the connection / its customer link / its cut-over existed, an invoice approved by an import that inserts approved rows, and a document the receiver's parser would refuse (a legacy negative line) are NOT sent, and nothing re-sends them. A re-send is P5: its reconciliation lists the unsent documents (approved customer documents on an active connection with no message). There is no re-send feature in P2.
- The inbox routes (`pages/api/accounting/intercompany/inbox*`) call the receiver services inside `withAuth` as the session company; `acceptMessage(userId, companyId, messageId, input, { auto })` is the one accept, for a person's click and for the worker.

### Business rules
- **Messages only from the sender.** The sender writes its own outbox row and, for a GROUP pair, its own side of `intercompany_transactions` (one `sale` row, `unmatched`; a credit note is negative); it never writes in, or reads, the receiver's books. The sender's customer decides who is the partner (the side's `customer_id`, one connection per company; one indexed query on every approval); a message needs an ACTIVE connection, a cut-over date (none = nothing mirrors) and `document date >= cut-over`. One message per (from company, source doc type, source doc id, kind): an existence check first, the DB's UNIQUE behind it. A foreign-currency document IS sent (its currency is in the payload); the receiver refuses to accept it.
- **A message carries its source document (database, migration 1013).** `intercompany_payload_problem`, called by the messages guard on INSERT: the source is APPROVED (invoice: approved / sent / partially_paid / paid / overdue; note: approved / partially_applied / applied) and the payload's number, date, currency, subtotal, VAT, total, line count and the multiset of line amounts, and `senderName` / `senderVatNumber` (cut at 200 / 50 characters) are the sender's books'. A sender cannot send amounts, a name or a VAT number that are not its own. The receiver's parser then bounds each line's VAT to its amount at its rate (within a cent: header VAT is split over the lines by `allocateLineVat`, largest remainder, like a ledger, whatever the number of lines) and a credit note is exactly one line.
- **The sender builds what the receiver can read.** Text is cut at the parser's limits (description 1 000, notes 2 000, name 200, VAT number 50), an empty description becomes "(no description)"; a line that does not multiply out (a percent-discounted line) is one unit at its amount. IsaFlow refuses negative quantities, prices and discounts (`priceDraftLines`) and has no header discount, so none is carried; the sender runs the receiver's parser on what it built and does not send what fails it (logged at error).
- **Accept = DRAFT only; nothing is approved or posted**, in ONE savepoint (a refusal, answered 4xx, commits nothing). It locks the message row; refuses a handled, dismissed or WITHDRAWN message; re-checks the connection (active), the membership of a group pair (`intercompany_in_one_group`) and, for the worker, the side's `auto_accept` AND that the connection is a group; needs the supplier linked on THIS company's side; refuses a duplicate before writing (a bill: this supplier's number; a credit note: an earlier accepted message from the same sender with the same number whose note still exists and is not cancelled: read from the immutable message, never from the editable reason); chooses an account per line (the person's choice, else the account last used for the same sender description on this connection's accepted bills, case and spacing ignored, else the side's default), all checked to be this company's, active, non-header (`accountsUsable`, then the bill's own account rules); creates the draft through `createSupplierInvoice` (`trustSourceTax`: the sender's exact line VAT; `externalRef` is NOT set, so the unique supplier + number index still catches a repeat) or `createCreditNote` (unlinked; the sender's exact VAT); marks the message accepted with its target; records the mirror link (`origin = mirror`, active); then, last and best effort, copies the sealed PDF. A draft made by auto-accept says so ("Created by intercompany auto-accept" in the bill's reference and notes, the note's reason).
- **The group row matches on APPROVAL, not accept.** A draft books nothing, and `autoGenerateEliminations` eliminates every `matched` row, so `intercompany_sync_group_row` (definer, called by the approve / cancel services) matches the sender's row only while the receiver's bill / supplier credit note is approved (target journal set, variance = the sender's amount less the receiver's subtotal, `variance` when it differs, which eliminates nothing) and sets it back to unmatched when the document is cancelled. A deleted draft stays unmatched. `intercompany_transaction_parties_immutable` lets `target_journal_entry_id` be set once, to a journal of the target company, or cleared with an unmatch.
- **The sender cancels.** `intercompany_withdraw_for_source` (definer; the session company, a signed-in user of it, a document really cancelled in its books) WITHDRAWS a pending / failed / dismissed message (status `withdrawn`: not acceptable, never auto-accepted, under Handled as "Cancelled by sender") and only stamps `source_cancelled_at` on an accepted one, whose inbox row then reads "Sender cancelled this document" while the receiver's draft and data stay untouched. A rejected message is left alone. The guard takes these system updates only from migration_admin with the transaction-local flag `app.intercompany_system_update`.
- **PDF copy.** Bytes are read through `downloadCapturedDocument(bucket, path, <sender company id>)`, which refuses a path outside the SENDER's prefix, must hash to the payload's `sha256`, and are stored under the RECEIVER's prefix as a captured document (`source_document_id` of the bill). A failure leaves the draft without it; a file stored for an accept that then fails is removed (best effort, logged). The upload cannot be moved before the row lock (the document id is not known yet).
- **Reject** needs a reason (trimmed, at most 2000; the DB also requires it) and goes back to the sender's outbox row; it touches nothing else. **Dismiss** sets aside, **restore** brings it back (needs an active connection) as a message only a person handles (`manual_only`). Accepted, rejected and withdrawn are final. Viewers read only (`withWriteAccess` and the guard).
- **Auto-accept worker** (`autoAcceptWorker.ts`, `POST /api/cron/intercompany-auto-accept`, `scripts/isaflow-intercompany-auto-accept.{service,timer}`, every 10 minutes; installing the units is an ops step). It has no user and no company, and `intercompany_in_one_group` is false without a signed-in user, so it has its OWN definer path: `intercompany_auto_accept_queue(limit, claim)` (owner migration_admin; EXECUTE for app_service and migration_admin only, never app_user) lists pending invoice / credit-note messages whose RECEIVER side switched `auto_accept` on BEFORE the message was created (`auto_accept_since`, set by the database: switching it on never drains a backlog), on an ACTIVE GROUP connection whose companies still share a current active group (`intercompany_shared_group`), never a restored message (`manual_only`), with one write-role user of the receiver in good standing (owner, then admin, then manager; `users.is_active`, status active, not suspended; company not suspended / deleted). It returns ids and the attempt number only, at most 10 per receiver per call, and with `claim` stamps each attempt (`auto_attempts`, `auto_next_attempt_at`: back-off 1, 2, 4 ... 60 minutes, at most 8 attempts), so one pair cannot starve the others and a message that keeps deferring never blocks the head. The queue runs on the SERVICE pool's login; every message then runs in `withReceiverTx` on the REQUEST pool's login (the service login inherits app_service, whose policies are USING (true): a RESET ROLE inside the transaction would read every tenant, while isaflow_app holds no table privilege): app_user, the claims of THAT user and the RECEIVER's company GUC, one transaction per message, lock_timeout 5 s, statement_timeout 60 s, running `acceptMessage(..., { auto: true })`: the same service, guards and RLS as a click. `handled_via = 'auto'` is set by the transaction-local GUC `app.intercompany_handled_via` (the guard honours it only with the side's flag on an active group connection). A refusal marks the message `failed`: its `error` (readable by the SENDER) is a fixed sentence of ours (`ConnectionRefusal.safeForSender`) or the generic "Could not create the draft; the receiving company must review it."; only the SQLSTATE and the error class are logged (never the message: it can carry values from rows). A transient lock / serialisation fault leaves it for the back-off; a deferral on the 4th attempt or later is `stuck`, logged at error, and makes the cron answer 500. Auto-accept creates DRAFTS (the side form says "they go through your normal approval"): it never approves.
- A message accepted by the worker is attributed to the chosen write-role user (`handled_by`, the draft's `created_by`) with `handled_via = 'auto'` and the draft says so.
- Limits: recurring-invoice approvals by a user without a write role send no message; a message whose PDF could not be sealed at approval carries no PDF; the group row of a credit note is negative (the eliminations treat it as a negative sale).
