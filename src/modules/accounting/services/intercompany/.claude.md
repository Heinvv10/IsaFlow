# intercompany/

Intercompany mirror posting (plan: `docs/plans/2026-11-17-intercompany-mirror-posting.md`). P1 = group connections (migrations 1000, 1002). P3 = the pre-cut-over pairing screen (`pairing*.ts`, migration 1012): it links already-open documents and writes no posting. **P2 (migration 1019) = the invoice + credit-note mirror**: an approved customer invoice / customer credit note leaves a message in the SENDER's outbox, the RECEIVER accepts it in the Bills > Intercompany inbox (a DRAFT supplier invoice / supplier credit note), and the auto-accept worker does the same as the receiver for a group side that asked for it. Tables: `intercompany_connections`, `intercompany_connection_sides`, `company_connection_keys`, `intercompany_messages`, `intercompany_document_links` (keys and the payment / allocation kinds wait for P4 / P6).

## Modules
- `connectionService.ts` — barrel plus `createGroupConnection`, `revokeConnection`, `connectionView`. `connectionSettingsService.ts` — `updateConnectionSettings` (the pair's agreement). `connectionSideService.ts` — `updateOwnSide`. `connectionRead.ts` — `listConnections`, `listGroupCandidates`. `connectionOptions.ts` — the side form's pick-lists.
- `connectionValidation.ts` — pure: request shapes (`parseCreateInput`, `parseSideInput`, `parsePairInput`), `autoAcceptRefusal`, `orderedPair`, `computeChecklist`. `connectionTypes.ts` — types and `ConnectionRefusal` (status 400/403/404/409). `connectionRoute.ts` — the routes' answer for a refusal.

- `pairingTypes.ts` — types. `pairingMatch.ts` — pure: `normaliseNumber`, `matchDocuments` (number + total to the cent; same number with another total is a mismatch; an amount alone never matches; foreign currency is `unsupported`). `pairingValidation.ts` — request shapes. `pairingRead.ts` — this company's own documents and links, and the two definer reads of the partner. `pairingView.ts` — `buildPairingView`, `blockReason`. `pairingWrite.ts` — `createPairings`, `confirmPairing`, `unlinkPairing`, `refusalFor`.

## Caller contract
Called by `pages/api/accounting/intercompany/*` inside `withAuth`: `sql` is the request's transaction as app_user with the session company's GUCs, so RLS limits every read and write to the session company. Service functions take the session `companyId` (and `userId` where a role check needs it). A refusal is thrown as `ConnectionRefusal` before anything is written; a refused write inside a savepoint (`withSavepoint`) does not poison the request's transaction.

## Main function signatures
- `listConnections(companyId, connectionId?)` — this company's connections, each with the pair `settings`, `canEditSettings` (write role in both companies), ITS OWN side and setup checklist only.
- `createGroupConnection(userId, companyId, partnerCompanyId)` — write role in both; both current members of one group (asked of `intercompany_in_one_group`, the definer helper); a revoked group connection is re-activated by one `UPDATE ... WHERE status = 'revoked'`, never duplicated; a concurrent create is a 409.
- `updateConnectionSettings(userId, companyId, connectionId, input)` — mode / cut-over / interest, write role in BOTH companies, not once revoked.
- `updateOwnSide(companyId, connectionId, input)` — partial update (absent key unchanged, null clears) of the session company's side; upsert on (connection, company).
- `revokeConnection(companyId, connectionId)` — either party.

## Business rules
- The pair is stored ordered (`company_low_id < company_high_id`, `orderedPair`); at most one LIVE (not revoked) row per pair (partial unique index). Kind never changes. External connections cannot be created until P6 (the guard refuses the INSERT).
- Settlement mode, cut-over date and interest rate belong to the PAIR (the connection row), not to a side. A group connection's change needs a write role in both companies (service and `intercompany_connections_guard`).
- A company never reads or writes its partner's side: RLS is `company_id = session company`. Customer/supplier must be the company's own (non-deleted) and linked to one connection per company (partial unique indexes: a second link is a 409); loan and default expense accounts its own, active and non-header (`isHeaderSql`). The database re-checks ownership in `intercompany_sides_guard`, and refuses any change once the connection is revoked.
- `auto_accept` only on a `group` connection, and only while both companies are in one group: `autoAcceptRefusal` in the service, `intercompany_sides_guard` (ENABLE ALWAYS trigger) in the database.
- A revoked connection releases its sides' customer and supplier links (`intercompany_release_links`, a registered definer fired by the status change, including the group-leave revocation); re-activating does not restore them.
- Group membership holds for the life of a group connection, and the group must be ACTIVE (`company_groups.is_active`, which `deleteGroup` clears). `intercompany_in_one_group(a, b)` (SECURITY DEFINER, registered in `app_definer_reviews`) is checked on create, re-activate, message insert and auto-accept. It answers only for a signed-in user of one of the two companies (never the session's company GUC), so it is false when `auth.uid()` is NULL and the P2 auto-accept worker needs its own definer path; it is no membership oracle. A pair that does share a group takes the two companies' rows of `intercompany_company_locks` (closed to every app role, one row per company, made by a trigger on `companies`) FOR SHARE until commit; a join takes ALL the group's members' rows FOR SHARE; the changers are STATEMENT-level triggers with transition tables on `company_group_members` and `company_groups` (UPDATE and DELETE) that take their whole set (the leavers, the parties of the connections the change would end, the members connected to a leaver) FOR UPDATE NOWAIT all or nothing, retrying, so a waiting changer holds nothing and a multi-row statement locks one set; the wait is bounded (`app.intercompany_changer_budget_ms`, default 10 s) and then fails with SQLSTATE 55P03, which withErrorHandler answers 503 + Retry-After from any route. They never return before locking except for a change that changes nothing. The protocol needs READ COMMITTED: `intercompany_in_one_group`, every changer, the joiner and the sides guard refuse with 40001 at REPEATABLE READ / SERIALIZABLE (as migration 886 does for `company_users`). A leave, a bulk leave, a deactivation or a group deletion at the same moment waits and then revokes what was created, two of them serialise, and a stranger can hold nothing (lock protocol in the 1002 header). app_user has no table-level UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE on the group tables (it could `LOCK TABLE` them): UPDATE is granted by column, to what `groupCrudService` writes; a new writer of another column needs a column grant in a migration. They revoke the active group connections whose companies no longer share a current active group (`revoked_by` = `system:group-membership`, accepted by the connections guard only from migration_admin with a transaction-local flag) and switch their sides' `auto_accept` off. Whatever writes on a connection later (the P2 auto-accept worker) re-checks through the same helper at action time, not on its own read of `company_group_members`.
- Viewers read only: every app_user write on the five tables needs `intercompany_user_can_write(<acting company>)` in the guard (status changes, sides, sending and handling a message, links, keys).
- Messages: UNIQUE (from company, source doc type, source doc id, kind); for the allocation and payment kinds the source doc is the allocation / payment ROW id; inserted only on an ACTIVE connection between its two companies; the sender only inserts, the receiver moves a pending message on (a dismissal can be restored, accepted and rejected are final, accepting, rejecting and restoring need the connection active; an invoice / credit_note / interest_invoice names a source document of the SENDING company that fits its kind, and an accepted one names a target of the RECEIVING company that fits it: supplier invoice, or supplier credit note for a credit note; the kinds that create no document carry no target; a receiver document is the target of ONE accepted message per RECEIVING COMPANY, whatever the connection; a side's first save, a message send and a link insert read their connection FOR SHARE); `handled_via` is `auto` only when the session sets `app.intercompany_handled_via = 'auto'` AND the receiver's side has auto-accept on an active group connection; `error` holds a generic sentence, never raw SQL or exception text.
- Links (`intercompany_document_links`) are the authoritative pair record: a mirror link is made by the receiver of an accepted message and joins exactly its source and target documents (active at once; both are re-checked against the message's two companies, so a forged message row cannot link a third company's document); a manual pairing starts `pending_confirmation`, names a document that exists in the inserter's own company, and goes active when the other party confirms (a user with a write role in both companies of a group may confirm both); either party may unlink. A company document is in one live link at a time whatever the connection and the position (`intercompany_document_slots`, kept by a definer trigger: an active link holds both documents, a pending pairing only the side its inserter confirmed, so a partner cannot squat a document by proposing it; 1002 refuses to apply over duplicates; active links and their slots survive a revoke until a party unlinks); confirming needs the connection active and the confirmed side's document to exist in its company; one mirror link per message.

## P2: the mirror (messages in, drafts out)
Files: `mirrorPayload.ts` (PURE: the payload's types, `parseMirrorPayload` = the receiver's untrusted-input check, `payloadBlocker` = ZAR only; the payload itself is built by the DATABASE), `outboxService.ts` (the sender: `mirrorApprovedInvoice`, `mirrorApprovedCreditNote`, each one call of `intercompany_send_for_source`), `mirrorHooks.ts` (`withdrawForCancelledDocument`, `syncGroupRowFor`), `inboxRead.ts` / `inboxTypes.ts` / `inboxValidation.ts` (the receiver's list, counts, request shapes; PURE, it owns `checkReason`, which `handleService.ts` re-exports), `acceptContext.ts` (this company's side, account suggestions, duplicate lookup), `acceptBlockers.ts` (PURE), `acceptDocuments.ts` (the draft + the PDF copy), `acceptService.ts` (`previewAccept`, `acceptMessage`), `handleService.ts` (reject / dismiss / restore / `markMessageFailed`), `linkOrder.ts` (PURE), `receiverTx.ts` + `autoAcceptWorker.ts` (the worker; it stops starting messages after `AUTO_ACCEPT_BUDGET_MS`, under the timer's `curl --max-time`), `mirrorErrors.ts`.

### Caller contract
- `approveCustomerInvoice` calls `mirrorApprovedInvoice(companyId, invoiceId)` after the PDF seal; `approveCreditNote` calls `mirrorApprovedCreditNote` for a CUSTOMER note and `syncGroupRowFor` for a SUPPLIER one. `cancelCustomerInvoice` / `cancelCreditNote` (customer) call `withdrawForCancelledDocument`; `approveSupplierInvoice` / `cancelSupplierInvoice` / `cancelCreditNote` (supplier) call `syncGroupRowFor`. All run on the caller's transaction inside a savepoint and NEVER throw: a failure is logged AT ERROR (SQLSTATE only) and the document change stands. The hooks maintain INBOX state (withdraw / stamp) and the group row's DISPLAY columns only, and never block: no group lock, and each definer bounds its lock waits at 2 s TIGHTEN-ONLY (it reads `lock_timeout`, sets 2 s only when the caller's is looser or none, and puts the caller's value back at every exit; a function-level `SET lock_timeout` would REPLACE a tighter bound such as the one `cancelSupplierInvoice`'s `underFlowLockBudget` holds), so a wait on a held row lock ends in a logged error, not a hang. No figure depends on a hook having run: the derived eliminations find the receiver's journal from the accepted message's bill when the sync never bound the row, and accept itself asks the ledger whether the sender's document is still in its books (`intercompany_message_source_out`), so a hook that timed out cannot let a person accept a cancelled sale.
- **Never-mirrored cases, and the re-send.** A mirror that fails (the database refuses, a session without a write role, a revoked connection), an approval that happened before the connection / its customer link / its cut-over existed, an invoice approved by an import that inserts approved rows, and a document the receiver's parser would refuse (a legacy negative line) are NOT sent, and nothing re-sends them. A re-send is P5: its reconciliation lists the unsent documents (approved customer documents on an active connection with no message). There is no re-send feature in P2.
- The inbox routes (`pages/api/accounting/intercompany/inbox*`) call the receiver services inside `withAuth` as the session company; `acceptMessage(userId, companyId, messageId, input, { auto })` is the one accept, for a person's click and for the worker.

### Business rules
- **The sender does not author the payload (database, migration 1019).** A document message is written by `intercompany_send_for_source(doc type, doc id)` (definer; the session company, a signed-in write-role user): it reads the APPROVED customer invoice / customer credit note of that company (invoice: approved / sent / partially_paid / paid / overdue; note: approved / partially_applied / applied), its lines, the company's name and VAT number (trimmed) and currency (NULL = ZAR), builds the payload itself (`intercompany_build_payload`, the parser's rules, cents-exact; the header VAT is split over the lines by largest remainder, like a ledger, whatever the number of lines; text cut at the parser's limits, an empty description becomes "(no description)"; a line that does not multiply out is one unit at its amount; the PDF only when it is the one sealing wrote for THIS invoice: `<company>/<its captured document>.pdf` with that document's hash), writes the group row and the message (recording, in database-written columns, the group row's id `group_transaction_id` and the approval journal `source_journal_entry_id`: the definers below read those, never the payload's reference, and confirm the row is the sender's to that receiver for that journal), and returns `(message id, NULL, NULL)` or `(NULL, reason, detail)`: `no_connection` (one indexed query: the common answer), `no_cutover`, `before_cutover`, `forbidden`, `already_sent`, `not_found`, `not_approved`, `no_journal` (approved in status only: no journal that is the document's OWN posting and stands: the company's, an original entry, named for this document, linked by no other invoice / credit note and recorded on no other message; `intercompany_own_posting_problem`, detail says which), `unmirrorable` (a document the receiver's parser would refuse: a negative line, a header that is not its lines, a VAT more than a cent from its rate; logged at error, never sent). The messages guard REFUSES the sender's own INSERT of an invoice / credit-note message (app_user, app_service: 42501), so no payload field (line VAT, due date, notes, PDF, credited invoice, group reference) is the sender's to choose. The SQL builder is compared with the TypeScript reference (`tests/helpers/mirrorReference.ts`) on 300 random invoices in `mirror-build.int.test.ts`. A fixture that needs a message plants it as the table owner with the sender's claims (`plantMessage`).
- **Messages only from the sender.** The sender's session writes its own outbox row and, for a GROUP pair, its own side of `intercompany_transactions` (one `sale` row, ZAR only, `unmatched`; a credit note is negative; both through the definer above); it never writes in, or reads, the receiver's books. The sender's customer decides who is the partner (the side's `customer_id`, one connection per company); a message needs an ACTIVE connection, a cut-over date (none = nothing mirrors) and `document date >= cut-over`. One message per (from company, source doc type, source doc id, kind): an existence check first, the DB's UNIQUE behind it. A foreign-currency document IS sent (its currency is in the payload, no group row); the receiver refuses to accept it.
- **A changed source is visible (the class is open).** `intercompany_message_source_changed(message)` (definer, the message's receiver only) rebuilds the payload from the sender's books and compares it with the stored one: the inbox detail and the review dialog warn "The sender changed this document after sending it", and the auto-accept worker refuses such a message (it goes `failed` with that sentence). **Follow-up, not fixed here:** an approved customer invoice's lines, totals and number are still editable by a writer after the send (the pre-existing P6 class); a lock on approved documents belongs to the AR module, not to this one.
- **Accept = DRAFT only; nothing is approved or posted**, in ONE savepoint (a refusal, answered 4xx, commits nothing). It locks the message row; refuses a handled, dismissed or WITHDRAWN message; re-checks the connection (active), the membership of a group pair (`intercompany_in_one_group`) and, for the worker, the side's `auto_accept` AND that the connection is a group; needs the supplier linked on THIS company's side; refuses a duplicate before writing (a bill: this supplier's number; a credit note: an earlier accepted message from the same sender with the same number whose note still exists and is not cancelled: read from the immutable message, never from the editable reason); chooses an account per line (the person's choice, else the account last used for the same sender description on this connection's accepted bills, case and spacing ignored, else the side's default), all checked to be this company's, active, non-header (`accountsUsable`, then the bill's own account rules); creates the draft through `createSupplierInvoice` (`trustSourceTax`: the sender's exact line VAT; `externalRef` is NOT set, so the unique supplier + number index still catches a repeat) or `createCreditNote` (unlinked; the sender's exact VAT); marks the message accepted with its target; records the mirror link (`origin = mirror`, active); then, last and best effort, copies the sealed PDF. A draft made by auto-accept says so ("Created by intercompany auto-accept" in the bill's reference and notes, the note's reason).
- **The group row follows the LEDGERS, and a mirrored row is the database's to match.** `intercompany_sync_group_row` (definer, a write-role user of the receiver; called by the approve / cancel services, including the approve that reuses a journal an earlier "Post to GL" wrote) sets the sender's row from the receiver's ledger, never from a writable status or link column. **Unmatched:** the sender's journal the message records has been reversed (parity of its reversal chain; a stamp is only the withdrawal's record), or the bill is not approved AND the journal the row was first matched to is really out of the ledger. **Unchanged:** the journal the row was FIRST matched to (`first_target_journal_entry_id`, written by the definer, never cleared by a release) still stands: nothing the bill's columns say moves a bound row. **Matched:** the bill's journal is posted, standing, the receiver's own, the one posting THIS document wrote (`auto_supplier_invoice` / `auto_credit_note`, "Supplier invoice N" / "Credit note N"), no other document's posting, in the same FINANCIAL YEAR of the receiver's calendar as the sender's date, not another row's target, for the connection's supplier, and carrying the sender's figures by value (expense lines net to its amount, input VAT to its VAT, the payable to its total, no other kind of line). **Variance** otherwise, with `variance_reason` (the receiver sees it on its handled inbox row; "cut-off: different financial years" for a 30 Dec sale booked on 2 Jan; a capitalised cost stays a variance, it needs an unrealised-profit entry). The row's match columns are a DISPLAY value (the inbox's "group match", the variance reason): no figure is read from them.
- **No session matches a mirrored row.** A row a message records (`group_transaction_id`) can be changed (match_status, matched_by / at, variance) only by migration_admin (the trigger refuses app_user, app_service and the service login), and `matchIntercompanyTransactions` refuses it (`IntercompanyMirroredRowError`). The elimination generator skips every mirrored row (`intercompany_is_mirrored_row`), and NEVER skips a manual (P1) row because of a mirrored document: it drafts the row's elimination as always. **RULING 2026-11-21 (Hein), the per-pair cutover:** `intercompany_pair_cutovers(group)` gives each mirroring pair (an active group connection with a cut-over date and both sides' `mirroring_since`, set by the database while a side's customer and supplier links are both set) and its cutover, the latest of those dates. From it a sale-class row of the pair (either direction) is left to mirroring (`skipped`, "eliminated by mirroring") only through a STORED, exclusive link (`intercompany_mirror_links`, written only by the definer `intercompany_mirror_link(row, periodEnd)`): one active link per row and per (mirror, role), so a mirror accounts for at most one sale row and one purchase row across every run and period; a new link takes the row's candidate mirrors in closeness order (`intercompany_mirror_candidates`) and goes to the first one that is free and not closer to another unlinked row on or after the cutover that the mirror's pair stands for as at the end of that row's own month; a sender cancel is judged as at the period (the cancel stamp is never a filter), so a cancel reversed after the period leaves the period eliminated by the mirror; the function refuses a row before its pair's cutover, takes the generator's per-group lock and holds the date to [row date, today]; a link holds while its mirror matches the row and its pair stands as judged for the period (`intercompany_mirror_stands(row, asat)`: the pair stands at the later document date and at the later of that and LEAST(period end, today), and every revenue / expense line of the two journals is mapped in the group), else it is released (history kept) and the row's other candidates are tried (a cancel and re-issue inside the period: the re-issue takes the row); only when none takes it is the row drafted. No kind that carries or names a document is a session's to insert (invoice, credit note, interest_invoice, anything naming a customer invoice / credit note / bill).
- **The ledger's parity is the truth, in both directions, and it is judged BY VALUE.** `reversal_of_id` is a column a writer can set on a posted journal, so a link in a reversal chain counts only if it is posted, of the same company and its lines are the exact negation, account by account, of the journal it claims to reverse (`intercompany_lines_negate`); a forged, empty or re-pointed link ends the chain. Every reversal of a journal (the reversal service, MCP, a cancel) calls `intercompany_journal_chain_changed`: a journal a message or a row rests on, reversed, withdraws / stamps the message and releases the row; a reversal that is itself reversed revives the message and judges the row afresh (`intercompany_sync_core`: matched again while its document is still approved on the journal it was first matched to; a variance re-evaluated), but only while the document is LIVE in its books (approved and linked to that journal: `intercompany_document_live`). A cancelled invoice is never revived by a chain that looks alive again, and the send does not re-pend its message: a re-approved cancelled invoice is not re-sent; a new document gets a new message. A single-line invoice carries its header VAT whatever the line's rate says (an import); several lines are held within a cent of their rates.

## Pairing (P3, migration 1012)
- A pairing is a manual-pairing document link between this company's open customer invoice (direction `ar`) or bill (`ap`) and the partner's bill / invoice, both dated BEFORE the connection's cut-over date and open (not draft / pending approval / cancelled / deleted, total above amount paid), of the customer / supplier each side linked on the connection. It writes only `intercompany_document_links`: no journal, no GL row, no `intercompany_transactions` row.
- The partner's side comes only through two registered definers, never a table read: `intercompany_pairing_partner_documents(connection, 'customer_invoice'|'supplier_invoice')` (id, number, date, total, open amount, currency, and the link of this connection holding it; documents held by a link of another connection are left out) and `intercompany_pairing_partner_loan_balance(connection)` (one net-debit number, NULL when it cannot be given). Both answer only for a signed-in WRITER of the session company (`intercompany_user_can_write`, which reads `company_users` by `auth.uid()`: the company GUC alone proves nothing), on an ACTIVE connection with a cut-over date; for anyone else they are empty / NULL.
- A pending pairing reserves only the side its proposer verified (1002 slots): the partner's proposal naming this company's document does not hide it here, and a proposal whose document has since been paired another way is `conflict` and can only be declined. A user with a write role in BOTH companies of a group confirms both sides in one request; anyone else's pairing waits for the partner's `confirmPairing`. Either party may unlink (also on a revoked connection); an unlinked pair is offered again.
- `createPairings` is all-or-none (one request transaction: a database refusal aborts it whole, so no savepoint is needed) and records only the pairs the screen SUGGESTS right now (same number and total): an id from anywhere else, an amount mismatch or an unmatched document is a 409 before the database sees it. `confirmPairing` / `unlinkPairing` act only on this screen's own links (origin `manual_pairing`, invoice with bill); a P2 mirror link or a credit-note link is a 404 here.
- The loan difference is each company's loan-account net debit over posted journals dated before the cut-over (the partner's from the definer); `difference = mine + theirs` is nil when one is the other's mirror. Display only; nothing is posted to close it.
- Open amounts and "open" are CURRENT, not as at the cut-over: a document dated before the cut-over that has been paid since is no longer offered and needs no pairing. The partner's proposal naming one of this company's documents is listed under Pairings only, never also as an exception. The `?connection=` deep link is read once the router is ready.
