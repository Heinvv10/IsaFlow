## Recent changes

- 2026-10-09 (#678 review, Hein; round 2): added `project_fno_mismatch` (`dimensionChecks.ts`) — a carrier whose stored CC1 contradicts the FNO its CC2 derives through the CURRENT parent chain (a recursive walk mirroring `deriveFnoId`, company-scoped, cycle- and depth-bounded). It is how the journal-posting vs project-move race is closed: by detection, not a lock. Covered: posted journal lines in force AS AT `as_at` — a reversal pair dated by then is skipped whole (parity of the entry's depth in its reversal chain, found by a recursive company-scoped walk up `reversal_of_id`, capped at 16 — round 4; a mismatched line whose chain reaches the cap (16+ deep or cyclic) is FLAGGED with detail "on a reversal chain 16+ deep or cyclic — in-force state not judged", never dropped — round 5; an even step re-instates and is in force), and an entry reversed only AFTER `as_at` (by the reversal's entry_date, or `reversed_at` when there is no reversal row) is kept (round 3); UN-ALLOCATED bank lines with a CC1 stored (a CC2-only selection stores NULL by design; an allocated row's journal line is checked instead; removed feed rows out); supplier / customer invoice lines (`documentInPopulationExpr`, not deleted) and credit notes (not draft / cancelled) with a CC1 stored. Never flagged: a CC2 that derives NO FNO (a CC1 beside it is the customer FNO, written on purpose). Not covered: card defaults and tag-batch history (they block the move via `countCc2Carriers`). Every carrier is its own row — a document line and the journal line it posted both appear — so `amount` is tagging to repair, not money at stake; the finding says so. Fifteen checks now. Test: `tests/integration/dims-678-fno-drift.int.test.ts`.
- 2026-09-18 (L1): added `payment_matched_but_unapplied` — supplier bills carrying `payment_matched = true` while `amount_paid` does not cover them. Fourteen checks now. Not rewound to `as_at`: it is a contradiction between two columns of one row, not a balance at a date.

- 2026-11-11 (wave 5 ET, Hein ruling R-A): added `paye_payable_differs_from_emp201` (`payrollChecks.ts`): for each of the twelve months to `as_at` with a posted run journal, the net 2140 credit of posted `auto_payroll` journals dated in the month against `generateEMP201(month).payePayable` (ETI carry included). A difference means an earlier month in the ETI period changed after a later month booked its carry, or a hand journal. Sixteen checks now. Test: `tests/unit/services/gl/payePayableIntegrity.test.ts`.
- 2026-11-11 (wave 5 ET, review round 2): `paye_payable_differs_from_emp201` rebuilt: one ETI ledger for the twelve months (`loadEtiMonths`), the ledger side tied to the month by the run header link (`bookedByRunJournal.net2140`), the month's in-force directive payments and `ETI-YYYY-MM` references, not entry dates; only months ENDED by `as_at`; a month whose run was calculated before version 9 is named in the finding, not counted.
- 2026-11-11 (wave 5 ET, review round 3): `paye_payable_differs_from_emp201` reads the twelve months' run and ETI journals in two batched queries (`etiBooking.bookedRunJournalsByMonth` / `bookedEtiJournalsByMonth`), and compares 2140 with the EMP201 unfloored on both sides, so a negative-PAYE month compares like for like.
- 2026-11-12 (wave 5 ET, review rounds 4-5): the `paye_payable_differs_from_emp201` finding names only remedies that work. For a month with no pay run journal in force, "Book ETI for this month" on its EMP201 (or the `book_eti_for_month` connector tool), once the month has ended. For a month whose pay run journal is in force, reopen and approve the run again. (An ETI journal can no longer be reversed by hand: payroll/.claude.md, round 5.)
