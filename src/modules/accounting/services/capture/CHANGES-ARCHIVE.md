- 2026-10-25 **S-07e part B (migration 946): the mirror connections, as they stand.**
  - **Writes**: no app role writes `imap_mirror_connections` or `sent_mirror_connections` (SELECT only). Every write runs on migration_admin through `@/lib/auth/routeAdminPool` (`routeAdminQuery`; `routeAdminTx` for several statements): settings (`mirrorConnectionService`, `ar/sentMirrorAdmin`), scan stamps, the error flip (`mirrorScanError.ts`) and the due selection.   - **Scan stamps in a request** (scan-now, `mirrorScanNow.runScanNow`): queued behind one `runAfterCommit` per request (`afterRequestCommit` / `stampScan`), in order (a cursor stamp clears `last_error`, so the error flip must not run before it), so a rolled-back request never moves the cursor; a stamp made after the request finished is dropped with a warning. Outside a request they run at once. scan-now answers 409 (`ScanNowBusyError`) while a scan of the connection or another scan-now of the company runs, and runs under a 30 s deadline. The connection's PATCH, DELETE and (capture) `disconnect` routes answer 409 (`SCAN_ACTIVE_MESSAGE`, reason 'scan') while `isScanActive(id)`.

In a request the claim is freed by `releaseAfterRequestStamps` after the last queued stamp, at once on rollback, or by a 60 s backstop. In-process only (prod runs one process); `claimScan` is what keeps ticks from overlapping.

Residual: the cron is serial, so 20 companies with a drip-server each still make a tick up to 20 x 120 s; no per-tick time budget.
