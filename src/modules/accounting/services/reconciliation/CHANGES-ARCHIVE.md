## Recent changes
- 2026-10-02 (D7): a hand-reversed delete reversal no longer fails the AR/AP close checks. Mechanism 2 excluded only the `soft_delete_journal_reversals` entry R, so once R was reversed by hand R2 had no partner and was itemised as `posting_with_no_document` with an equal and opposite `unexplained_difference` on books that tied (first bad: ccd44bba3, when the close checks began reading the itemisation). It now excludes the deleted document's whole reversal chain; this also stops itemising a journal hand-reversed BEFORE its document was deleted. A live document's hand reversal is still itemised. Query moved to `orphanControlPostings.ts` for the 300-line rule. Tests: `tests/integration/k053-deleted-document-chain-reconciliation.int.test.ts`, `k053-aging-as-at-reversal-date.int.test.ts`; the L3 fixture's stray cent now rides on a draft credit note, since a forged sdjr row no longer excludes anything.
- 2026-09-30 (close-gate as-at): `buildControlBridgeItems` takes `asAtDate` and splits documents dated after it into a new `settlement_before_document` item (prepayments applied to later documents). On Velocity Fibre every one of 700 `document_not_on_control_account` items across 32 month-ends (May 2025 – Aug 2026, up to R5.2m a month) was this and none was a real cut-off error. The AR / AP close gates now read `reconcileSubledger` and tolerate the item. Tests: `tests/unit/mcp/w2/subledgerReconciliation.test.ts`, `tests/unit/services/gl/subLedgerTieCheck.test.ts`.
- 2026-09-19 (supplier refunds): a refund journal leaves `posting_with_no_document`. A supplier refund's DR Bank / CR Accounts Payable entry had no document to claim it, so it was counted here as a posting nothing owned. It now carries a `supplier_payments` row with a NEGATIVE `total_amount` (migration 681), which `shared/unappliedPaymentBasis` reports as a negative remainder and `unappliedPaymentCash` turns into a signed reconciling item. **The zero floor in `unappliedPaymentCash` was already written for this shape** — it caps the ALLOCATED figure, never the movement — so the item is the control movement itself, signed the way it moved the account, and the identity still holds. The one shape that cannot be documented is a deposit allocated straight to the AP control account: it credits the payable without naming a supplier, so the backfill reports it under `refund_on_control_unresolved` and mints nothing. One line on production, R120,290.00.
- 2026-09-19 (credit-note allocations): the document scan reads `credit_note_allocations` instead of `credit_notes.applied_amount` through the single link column, so a note applied FIFO over several documents relieves each of them by its own row. New `creditNoteRelief.ts` holds both arms and the proportional cap. On `isaflow_staging` AP for 4a159669 went from `document_not_on_control_account` −R3,728,724.75 (150 docs) against +R3,728,724.79 unexplained, to +R0.04 (4 docs) against R0.00, all three invariants holding; AR unchanged at R0.00. Pinned by `tests/integration/l3b-credit-note-allocations.int.test.ts` (service-driven shapes) and `l3c-credit-note-relief-caps.int.test.ts` (the proportional cap, the zero floor, the cancelled-with-rows guard and the as-at bound, each on a forged shape and each proved by mutation). The relief is dated by the note's `credit_date`, matching `agingRewind`, with the control-side cap still bounded by the journal's `entry_date`; dating it by the journal alone relieved the control side of a note the subledger had just rewound. The same commit fixed the L3 fixture, whose credit notes were auto-applying FIFO into bills other cases assert are open: on a faithful master checkout that left the AP residual at +R229.99 where the fixture builds −R0.01. (The fixture-repair commit message says R1,150.00; that came from an earlier run of the same check and did not reproduce. R229.99 is the figure a clean master reproduces.)
- 2026-11-13 (party ledger check): `partyLedgerCheckService` is the per-party, per-document counterpart of `subledgerReconciliationService` (MCP `party_ledger_check`). It reuses the statement services, `fetchARAgingInvoices` / `fetchAPAgingInvoices` (with the party filter), `agingRewind` and `getControlAccountBalances`; it adds only the document's own journal chain on the control accounts (`partyLedgerJournalLateral`) and the comparison (`partyLedgerClassify`, `partyLedgerTotals`). Its reason classes reuse this folder's vocabulary at document grain. Do not add a balance formula here: join the existing reader. Integration suite `tests/integration/party-ledger-check.int.test.ts`.
- 2026-09-18 (L3 round 2): measured against a full production copy (`isaflow_staging`). Three mis-detections found and fixed — cancelled documents still claiming their journals, credit notes relieving a document by their journal rather than their applied amount, and unapplied credit read off a status list. AP went from an unexplained R15,707,991.88 to R0.00, with `posting_with_no_document` falling from −R16,462,280.61 to +R124,704.80 and `document_not_on_control_account` from +R878,993.57 to +R0.04.
- 2026-09-18 (L3): control accounts resolved by SUBTYPE ONLY (the posting probe is gone); one shared definition of an unapplied payment; `unapplied_credit_note` itemised (AP's subledger carries no credit notes, so supplier credit fell into the residual unnamed); payment legs attributed by allocated amount; invariants reported. Verified on isaflow_dev: residual 0.00 on every AR and AP company, all three invariants holding, 144 previously-silent off-control payments named.
