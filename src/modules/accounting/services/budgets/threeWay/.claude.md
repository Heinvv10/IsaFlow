# budgets/threeWay/

Three-way budget engine (Budgets P6 PR A) and its DB service and routes (PR C), spec `docs/plans/budgets/P6-three-way-spec.md` §4, §5, §7, §10; **§13 overrides earlier sections**. Projects one version's budgeted P&L into a balanced balance sheet and a direct + indirect cash flow per month.

- `types.ts`: `EngineInput`, `EngineOutput`, `MonthResult`, `Buckets` (`BUCKET_KEYS`), `PnlMonth` (`PNL_BANDS`), `Posting`, `ThreeWayWarning`.
- `assumptionsSchema.ts`: zod `ThreeWayAssumptionsSchema` (`schemaVersion: 1`), `ScenarioOverridesDocSchema`, `StoredAssumptionsDocSchema`, `Prov<T>`. `assumptionsChain.ts`: `parseStoredDoc`, `parseAssumptions`, `mergeOverrides`, `resolveAssumptions`, `MAX_SCENARIO_DEPTH` (5). `assumptionsDefaults.ts`: `defaultAssumptions`.
- `daysMethod.ts` (`closingFromDays`, `walksPastStart`, `derivedDays`), `vatSchedule.ts` (`vatPeriodOf`, `vatDueMonth`, `vatSettleMonth`), `vatBook.ts`, `statutorySchedule.ts` (`payrollSplit`, `provisionalTaxApplies`, `sarsTopUpOffset`, `provisionalTaxSchedule`).
- `engine.ts` `runThreeWay(input): EngineOutput` (+ `engineInput.ts` validation/rates, `engineEvents.ts` dated events and 10c checks, `postings.ts`, `balanceSheet.ts`, `cashFlow.ts`). `compare.ts` `compareVersions(entries)`.
- `months.ts`, `ratios.ts` (`SA_STANDARD_RATE_BP` = 1500, pinned to the reports module's SA rate by test), `errors.ts`.

Money on the wire is CENTS.

## Caller contract (the DB service fills `EngineInput`)
- Money is integer CENTS. Buckets are DEBIT-POSITIVE (liabilities and equity negative); P&L bands natural sign (expenses positive). Months `'YYYY-MM'`; `months` = the version's whole fiscal year (`versionMonths`), 1..15 contiguous; month 1 = FY month 1, last = year end, prior year end = the month before.
- `pnl` aligned with `months`, bands by canonical subtype (§13.6). `lookback` oldest first, contiguous, ending the month before `months[0]` (ex VAT: turnover, costOfSales, supplierPurchases = stock purchases + supplier opex); ≥ 12 wanted, a short one warns `lookback_short` only when the days walk past it.
- `opening.buckets` must balance within 1 cent (`OpeningUnbalancedError` beyond; a 1-cent difference goes to retained earnings with `opening_cent_difference`). Retained earnings includes derived RE. `opening.unmapped` lists accounts no bucket rule claimed.
- `assumptions` is the RESOLVED doc (`resolveAssumptions` first); the engine re-validates it. `loanSchedules` keyed by existing `loanId` (`previewSchedule`) or planned `id` (`amortise`); only included existing loans and every planned loan repay. `checks.assetRegisterDepreciation` optional, aligned with `months`.
- `resolveAssumptions(targetId, versions, derived)`: `versions` holds every version of the chain with its parsed doc (or null); `derived` is the derive service's doc. Refusals are `AssumptionChainError` (`VERSION_NOT_FOUND`, `BASE_MISSING`, `CROSS_COMPANY`, `CROSS_YEAR`, `CYCLE`, `TOO_DEEP`, `OVERRIDES_ON_NON_SCENARIO`); a bad doc is `AssumptionsDocError` (`issues[]`).

## Service rules (PR C)
- Opening (10a): the day before the first month; after today → today, provisional. Buckets by `bucketOf` (§13.10; a loan's `gl_account_id`/`current_portion_account_id` → loans, the system RE account → retainedEarnings, liability bank/cash → otherCurrentLiabilities, `equity`/`share_capital` → shareCapital, `dividends` → retainedEarnings, `opening_balance_equity` → otherEquity). Income-tax and dividends payable open at 0 (no subtype, and the schema has no account selector). The cash check reports, per account, cash posted where the two account sets differ (headers: getCashBalance counts every level).
- Lookback supplier purchases = cost of sales + other opex (actuals are P&L only: stock movement taken as nil). Lookback months after a provisional as-at read 0.
- Assumptions write: `holdYearRegistry` → savepoint → version `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` (NOT bumped; open grid tabs stay valid) → 404/409 LOCKED → parse → token check (null = no row) → INSERT / UPDATE `WHERE updated_at = expected` → overrides: chain resolved AS WRITTEN (refusal rolls the write back) → one audit row (entity 'budget', version id, one field per changed section, old/new JSON). Token = the row's `updated_at` (UTC ISO microseconds).
- Chain: `readChain` walks `based_on_version_id` within the company only (≤ 7 rows); a base elsewhere reads as BASE_MISSING. Root with no document → the derived defaults.

## Business rules
- Every posting asserts Σdr = Σcr and a cash line names its `CashGroup`; receipts, supplier payments and stock purchases are RESIDUALS of the days-method closings, VAT-inclusive = ex VAT + VAT, payroll net = payroll − statutory: rounding never unbalances. One rounding per product, half away from zero, BigInt.
- VAT (§5 step 11): `vatPeriodOf` = `vatPeriodHelper` semantics ('odd' = periods ENDING in odd months); payable m+1 after the period end, refund at end + `refundLagMonths`, never before month 1. Opening VAT control joins the period of the month before the start; on payment basis less the VAT still inside opening AR/AP. Payment-basis VAT on cash = gross × s·r/(1 + s·r) (spec's s·r/(1 + r) equals it only at s = 1). Not registered: no VAT lines; opening VAT carried flat. Claimable capex VAT is at the full rate; non-claimable capex carries no VAT line.
- Payroll statutory share accrues and is remitted the next month (the liability held at the start of a month is paid in it).
- Provisional tax (10b + §13.1-3): only when `incomeTax.mode` is provisional AND the entity is pty_ltd/ltd/cc/npc (else `provisional_tax_not_applicable` when mode is on). IRP6 #1 = 50 % of FY tax in month 6 when the year has > 6 months; #2 = the balance in the last month; never negative. Top-up = a credit opening payable, `topUpMonthOffset` after the prior year end (default `sarsTopUpOffset`: 7 after a February YE, else 6), when inside.
- Indirect CF: NP + depreciation − ΔAR − ΔInv + ΔAP + Δpayroll + ΔVAT + Δtax, where ΔVAT excludes capex VAT (investing cash). Direct operating = indirect operating (I3).
- Dated entries outside `months` are skipped and warned (`outside_months`); 10c checks warn when > 5 % of the budgeted figure apart, never override. Negative cash is shown and warned, never funded.
- Scenario merge: objects deep-merge; a `Prov` and every list replace whole; lists `[]` clear.
- Compare: Δ = version − first, Δ% = Δ / |first| × 100 (null when first is 0); favourable: cash, min cash, NP higher; AR, AP, net debt lower.
