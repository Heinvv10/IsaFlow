# Budget tracking engine (`services/budgets/tracking/`)

Budget vs Actual rebuilt (budgets P3 PR-A; spec `docs/plans/budgets/P3-tracking-spec.md`, contract `docs/plans/budgets/00-shared-contract.md`). One engine for the BvA page (PR-B), its CSV/XLSX export, and from PR-C `getBudgetVariance`, the comparator, packs and MCP.

## Caller contract
- `buildBudgetTrackingReport(companyId, params, now?)` → `BudgetTrackingReport` (`trackingTypes.ts`). Queries are SEQUENTIAL (one pg client under withAuth). Refusals: `TrackingError` (400/404/409) or P1's `BudgetVersionError`, answered by `@/lib/budgetTrackingResponse`. Registry misses on any path go through `mapRegistryMiss` (4xx); invariant breaks (`BudgetGrainContradictionError`, `TrackingOrderError`) are logged 500s.
- `buildRangeTracking(companyId, startDate, endDate, opts?)` (`trackingRange.ts`, PR-C) → `RangeTracking`: the engine over a DATE RANGE, behind `getBudgetVariance`. `opts`: `versionId`, `cc1Id`, `cc2Id`, `buId`, `costCentreId` (either level), `wholeMonthsOnly` (a range that splits a budget month has no window budget). The facade's two modes: default = P0's partial-window sum, no filter, no version, no added field; `partialWindow: 'absent'` = this engine's rule and verdicts (the statement comparator).
- Every `TrackingLine` carries `budgetVerdict` ('budgeted' / 'absent' / 'partial' / 'above-filter' / 'unbudgeted'): the ONE place the line's budget is judged. `buildNotices` and the facade read it; nothing re-derives it.
- Routes: `GET /api/accounting/reports-budget-tracking` (JSON) and `GET /api/accounting/budget-tracking-export?format=csv|xlsx` (same query, zod in `trackingQuery`). Read-only, no withWriteAccess (decision 15); rate limited per company.
- Money: rands to the cent (integer cents, `budgets/grid/cents.ts`); `null` = absent, never 0. Band keys, labels and signs are the grid's (`budgets/grid/subtotals.ts`).
- `trackingActualRead.ts` is THE actuals SQL of the budgets programme (P4/P5/P6 read through it); P2's `priorActuals.ts` delegates to it (PR-B).
- `window.postedThrough` = P0's figure: latest posted, non-year-end entry date in the window, COMPANY-WIDE, any account, so it can be forward-dated. `window.inProgress.actualsTo` = latest entry date the FILTERED actuals read consumed IN the in-progress month (income-statement accounts, forward-dated included); null when that month has none.

## Consumers
- The page (`pages/accounting/reports/budget-vs-actual.tsx` + `components/accounting/budgets/tracking/`, PR-B) reads the wire shape `window.inProgress.{month, actualsTo}` + `window.postedThrough` (not the spec's `inProgress.postedThrough`) and sends only the parameters its period takes (`trackingControls.trackingSearch`): `fiscal_year` with month/custom, `quarter` outside quarter, `from`/`to` outside custom are 400s.
- Rows: `statementRows/budgetTrackingRows.ts` emits `statementModel`'s `Comparison.absent` (+ `absentReason` 'partial' / 'above-filter'), `StatementRow.projection`; the grid prints "—" with the ½ / ↑ marker and tooltip ("Budget covers only part of this window" / "Budget set above this filter").
- Browser bundle: the page imports `trackingByMember`, `budgetBridgeSteps` and `trackingProjection`, so nothing on their import chain may import `@/lib/neon` (`dayBefore` comes from `lib/dateUtils`, its one definition, never through `fiscalYearRegistry`). Pinned by `tests/unit/guards/bvaPageClientImports.test.ts`.
- PR-C: `getBudgetVariance` (`reports/budgetVarianceService.ts` + `budgetVarianceTracked.ts`) and the statement comparator read `buildRangeTracking`.
- `notices.noVersion` / `noVersionYears` mean "NO BUDGET IN THIS WINDOW", not "no version" (the year may have a version, even one holding budget outside the window). Word it "No budget for FY2026 in this window". `version` and `versionsByFy[y]` carry `empty: true` for such a year; the export prints "Original · approved (no budget in this window)".

## Files
- `trackingWindow.ts`: month / quarter (3-month blocks from the FY start; 14 months → Q5 of 2) / ytd / fy / custom (month-granular, ≤ 24 months), from the registry. Not calendar months: 409; unplaceable year: 400 (no start month: 409); off-period parameter or year outside 1900-2200: 400 (`assertParamsApply`).
- `trackingBudgetRead.ts`: versions (primary per year, or a named one: 404 foreign, 400 if the window reaches another year) built from `window.years`; amounts at their stored grain; `budgetAbsentYears`. `trackingFilter.ts`: ids under company_id (404), `cc1_id` must be a CC1 and `cc2_id` a CC2 (400), `readMemberOrder`.
- Pure: `trackingDimRollup.ts` (included / coarser / other), `trackingLines.ts`, `trackingBands.ts`, `trackingProjection.ts`, `trackingByMember.ts`, `budgetBridgeSteps.ts`.
- `trackingExport.ts`: CSV/XLSX of the copy-only `statementRows/budgetTrackingRows`; text guarded (`csvVal`, `neutralizeCsvCell`); a cross-FY Version line names each year ("FY2027: Original · approved; FY2028: no budget in this window"); notice lines explain blank Budget columns. Charts: `components/accounting/charts/varianceHeatmapOption.ts` + `VarianceHeatmap.tsx` ("Budgeted above this filter" vs "No budget for this month").
- `trackingCore.ts` (PR-C): the steps the report and the range share: budget rows → chains (one query) → budget filter → actuals → classes → absent years → lines with verdicts. `trackingRange.ts` (PR-C): `buildRangeTracking`; day-level actuals over the exact days, whole budget months, P1's `primaryVersionScopeAndYears` per year (a mid-month registry year is read, not 409'd; the BvA page's windows still refuse it), the year holding the range's end judged for the absent rule even when P1 left it no month, `splitsBudgetMonth` (a start not the 1st, an end not a month end, or a registry year starting or ending mid-month inside the range), no 24-month cap (the comparator route caps its own), no members; its projection plan is the range itself dated at its own end (it keeps a line with a budget in the range; nothing else reads it). `namedVersionScopeOf` (`trackingBudgetRead`) is the named-version rule both use.

## Rules (spec §2)
- Var R = actual − budget; `favourable` by polarity (income ≥ 0, expense ≤ 0, profit totals ≥ 0); Var % = variance / |budget|, null on 0. Bands and profit totals are computed here (GP = T − COS; OP = GP + OI − Opex; NP = OP − OE), never revenue + expense; a band with no budgeted line is absent.
- Actuals: posted, not `year_end`, revenue/expense, entry_date inclusive, EXPLICIT levels ANDed; `costCentre` is either-level (PR-C). Invariant 1 = income statement / dimension P&L to the cent. Lines print in Postgres' `ORDER BY account_code`.
- Budget rows: eff.cc1 = cc1 ?? FNO(cc2) (chains loaded once per request). Coarser (a named level NULL, nothing conflicting) is `unallocatedBudget`, never in Budget, never apportioned. A CC2 filter knows its FNO: that FNO's CC1 budget is coarser, another FNO's is other (deviation from the §2.4 table's literal text). Either level: included on `eff.cc1 = X OR eff.cc2 = X`, coarser only when both are NULL.
- D5: budgeted = an INCLUDED row in the window months; a zero budget prints 0, no row prints "—". Unbudgeted = no included row, none above the filter, not partial, actual ≠ 0. Notice figures are `{ income, expense }`, never one mixed sum; unallocated on GP / OP / NP is netted by polarity.
- Budget-absent year (`budgetAbsentYears`, from what the scoped read returns): no version, or no INCLUDED or COARSER income-statement row in that year's window months (other-class rows never count; coarser rows keep the year, as its unallocated footer). FY* outside the window is judged over its whole year. Any such year makes the WINDOW Budget / Var R / Var % and unallocated absent everywhere (lines, bands, members, footer; no bridge); `monthly[].budget` keeps the versioned months; nothing is called unbudgeted; the export adds "No budget for FYxxxx in this window".
- Partial budget (flagged for Hein): over a window touching MORE THAN ONE year, a line with included rows in some years' window months and none in another's, or a grain change between versions (included some months, only coarser in others), has no window Budget / Var R / Var % and `partialBudget` true (`budgetAboveFilter` when it has coarser rows). Its band and the profit totals above it have no budget, so no bridge; bands whose lines are whole keep theirs. Within ONE year D5 stands. `notices.partialBudget` = `{ count, actual }` (null when none); the export adds "N lines have a budget for only part of this window; their Budget is blank" (one line: "its Budget is blank").
- Members (`group_by`, unfiltered only): each member's net profit, the untagged remainder carrying every row without a member, so members partition NP. They follow NP: no budget under a budget-absent year, a partial line, or a member partial at its own grain (per member and account, the line rule). Order: masters' `ORDER BY code, id`, unknown ids after, untagged last.
- Projection: cutoff = min(window end, end of the month before today), clamped to [FY*.start − 1, FY*.end]; projected = actual to cutoff + budget after the cutoff's month; FY budget = every FY* month (absent per line only with no included row in the whole year). FY* = the year holding the window's end. A past month of an ended year projects as at the window end, not the FY actual (spec contradiction flagged for Hein).
- Projection month by month (PR-B r2 ruling, the lead's, for Hein to confirm; replaces r1's unbudgeted rule): over FY*, months up to the cutoff's month take their actual; months after it take the line's budget where it has a budget row for that month (a zero budget is a row), else the month's posted actual (forward-dated included; 0 if none). projectedFY = Σ; fyBudget / projVar unchanged (absent with no budget in FY*). A fully budgeted line is the §2.3 formula; an unbudgeted line projects its FY* actual; a partly budgeted one mixes. Why: the cutoff exists so a budget replaces part-month actuals; with no budget for a month it only dropped real postings (Projected FY below the Actual beside it: ledger-breaker r2 A1 forward-dated invoice, A2 year not started, B line budgeted Mar - Jun). The actuals read covers the whole of FY* (`readEndOf`); window, member and monthly figures still sum window months only, so a window can list a line with Actual 0 and its FY projection. Bands and totals carry `partialBudget` (a partial line is why their budget is absent). A forward-dated posting against a budgeted ZERO in a later month is replaced by that 0, so Projected FY can sit below the Actual beside it: the spec's formula for a budgeted month (a zero budget is a row). `projection.judgedThrough` = min(cutoff month, the month before today's) is the last complete month the heatmap judges: the cutoff alone is clamped up to FY*.start - 1 and would judge the month in progress on a window running into a future FY* (r3).

## Recent changes
- 2026-10-28: module created (P3 PR-A, greenfield), then hardened through seven review rounds; the rules above are the result.
- 2026-10-29 (P3 PR-B): the BvA page renders it; `priorActuals` delegates to the actuals read; the drill (`reports-account-transactions`) takes `cc1_id`/`cc2_id` and, for BvA only, `documents=1` / `sign=natural`. Review r1: an unbudgeted projection rule, replaced in r2 by the month-by-month rule above; the tooltip and export header say "a later month with no budget counts its posted actuals". r2: `TrackingBand.partialBudget`.
- 2026-10-29 (P3 PR-C): `getBudgetVariance` is a facade over `buildRangeTracking`; `trackingCore` shared with the report; each line's `budgetVerdict`; `splitsBudgetMonth` (the comparator's whole-month rule, R4). P0's `budgetWindowActuals.ts`, `loadWindowBudgets` and P1's `namedVersionScope` are deleted.
- 2026-10-29 (P3 PR-C review r2): `budgetAbsentYears` is judged BEFORE the dimension filter (R8): a year is absent only when its version has no income-statement budget row in the window months; `budgetOutsideFilterYears` / `notices.noBudgetAtFilterYears` name a year with a budget none of which is at the filter (every row another dimension's; coarser rows stay the unallocated footer's). A band whose only budget is above the filter makes the profit totals netting it null (R9, `Sums.hidden`); `sectionBudgets` gives P0's three sections the engine's budgets. The either-level `cost_centre_id` meaning is unchanged. `trackingCore` returns `outsideFilterYears`.
- 2026-10-29 (P3 PR-C review r3, FINAL R9): a band's budget (window AND FY) is NULL when a line in it is partial, when a line's budget sits only above the filter (strict, one is enough), or when the band has lines that speak about the window (an actual, a budget, a partial or above-filter one) and none has a budget; an unbudgeted line beside a budgeted one counts 0, a band with no lines counts 0 (and shows none). A profit total (and a P0 section) is NULL when any band it nets is null; absent when every band under it is empty. Cost: under a filter Net Profit is "—" whenever overheads are budgeted at company level or an account has no budget. A partial line is KEPT with no figure of its own (the BvA page used to drop it and show its band whole). `trackingNoticeText.ts` holds the one wording of the absent-year and budget-elsewhere notices (both pages and the export). `primaryVersionScope` is gone: `primaryVersionScopeAndYears` is the one walk.
- 2026-10-29 (P3 PR-C review r4, R11): the FY budget (`fyBudget`, `projVariance`) of a band and of the profit totals is judged over ALL the FY* months and every line the report keeps: a projection-only line counts as a line (a band with lines and no FY budget is null), and budget above the filter in ANY FY* month (`TrackingLine.fyBudgetAboveFilter`) blocks, even with no window cell (such a line is kept). The window rule is unchanged. `membersBudgetAbsent(bands)` (Net Profit has no budget) is the one rule for the group_by members' budgets. The BvA export's absent-year Notice is one row per year in `trackingNoticeText`'s words. The BvA page shows its notices in the empty state too, and its cross-year note names a year with no version.
