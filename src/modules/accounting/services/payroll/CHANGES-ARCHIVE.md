An entered 0 prints "0 hours worked" on the payslip (`payslipQuantities`). A severance-only leaver is not judged on hours. A change of pay basis, either rate or hours per day after calculation sends the run back (`entityChangeQuery` WORK_TERMS). **Figure change (M2): until ET lands, an hourly or daily line under 160 hours, or whose hours cannot be told, claims no ETI** (`ETI_HOURS_APPORTIONMENT_PENDING`). `PAYROLL_CALCULATION_VERSION` 5.
