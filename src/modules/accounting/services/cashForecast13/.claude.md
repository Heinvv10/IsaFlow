# cashForecast13/

13-week cash forecast (cash13). Product rulings: Hein 2026-11-16. Route `GET /api/accounting/cash-forecast-13` → `getCashForecast13(companyId, appToday())`.

## Engine (PURE: no `@/lib/neon`, no clock)
- `engine.ts` `runEngine(EngineInput): CashForecast13`. Order: known items → budget fill → manual items → overrides (array order).
- `weeks.ts`: 13 Monday–Sunday weeks, week 1 holds today. `weekIndexOf`: a date before today → week 1; after the last Sunday → null (left out, `beyond_horizon` warning).
- `lines.ts`: items fold into one line per `lineKey`; `sortLines` makes output independent of reader row order. `toCents` for every rand → cents.
- `budgetFill.ts`: per month and per category (customer receipts, supplier payments), budget − known in that month (by the item's EFFECTIVE date, today for overdue), floored at 0, spread by day (cumulative rounding), days before today dropped. Lines `budget:<category>`, kind `estimate`.
- `adjustments.ts`: manual items → `manual:<id>` (a repeat drops past occurrences; a past one-off is week 1); overrides never edit a source line: deltas go on `override:<lineKey>` (kind `override`); a bad override is skipped with `override_skipped`.
- Pure source rules: `arExpected.ts` (due / history = due + median days late with ≥3 paid in 12 months / no_history = week 1; disputes excluded and reported), `payDates.ts` (pay day or last working day, `backToFriday`), `recurringItems.ts` (`advanceDate`; a passed run is invoiced today).

## Invariants (tests/unit/services/cashForecast13)
- Integer cents everywhere (`assertCents` throws). Per week: opening + Σ lines = closing; next opening = closing. Line total = Σ weekly.

## Readers (SQL, read-only, every query company-scoped)
- `cashForecast13Service.ts` runs each source in `guardedSource` (own savepoint): a SQLSTATE error → `source_unavailable` warning; anything else propagates. Sequential, never `Promise.all` (savepoints nest on one connection).
- `readReceivables.ts`: `fetchARAgingInvoices` / `fetchAPAgingInvoices` (today, today). Paid date = MAX(`customer_payments.payment_date`) over allocations (not draft/cancelled); NOT `paid_at` (capture time).
- `readPayroll.ts`: latest `payroll_run_headers` with status approved/paid, `total_net`; a paid period is not paid again; statutory = max(0, PAYE − ETI) + UIF + SDL for EMP201 months not yet run.
- `readSars.ts` + `readVat.ts`: `getComplianceCalendarWithDB`, completed items excluded, pending past due → week 1 flagged `overdue_unfiled`. EMP201 `generateEMP201().totalDeductions`; VAT box 11 (closed), accrued + budget pro rata (open), budget only (future); refund at period end + `refundLagMonths`, last day of month. IRP6 only from a primary budget's `taxEvents` in the due month.
- `readBudget.ts`: flag `budgets_three_way` (`hasFeature`), `getThreeWay` per FY the horizon touches, each in a savepoint; typed three-way refusals → warning, not a crash.
- `readLoansRecurring.ts`: `previewSchedule` per active loan (own savepoint); recurring due dates use the RUNNER's terms (customer, else company default), not the template's `payment_terms` text.
