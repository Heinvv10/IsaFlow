# statement/

The one statement renderer and its parts. Every financial statement on screen
— income statement, balance sheet, AFS statements, management pack, report
pack — renders through `StatementGrid`.

## Contract: render, never compute

`StatementGrid` and `StatementRowView` add, subtract and derive NOTHING. They
take `StatementRow[]` and print it. The mapping from a report service to rows
lives in `src/modules/accounting/services/reports/statementRows/` — that is the
layer allowed to shape figures, and it copies rather than derives.

The one arithmetic here is `BalanceCheckBanner`'s `|A − (L + E)|`, which is a
CONTROL, not a statement figure: it exists to say whether the sheet balances
and is never presented as one of the report's own numbers.

## Files
- `StatementGrid.tsx` — the table. Builds one variance bar scale PER
  comparator from that comparator's own variances.
- `StatementRowView.tsx` — one row. Blanks the figure on `section` and `empty`
  kinds. With `row.href` the label is a `next/link` and the expand chevron is a
  SIBLING button; an anchor inside a button is invalid and loses one action.
- `VarianceBar.tsx`, `Sparkline.tsx` — pure marks. `Sparkline` filters
  non-finite points: one NaN makes min/max NaN and the polyline vanishes
  silently.
- `BalanceCheckBanner.tsx` — balanced / out-of-balance, shared by the balance
  sheet page and the statutory AFS Statement of Financial Position.

## Money

Figures go through `formatMoney` / `formatPct` from `@/lib/format/money`, never
`formatCurrency`. Two money formats on one statement is what the shared grid
exists to stop.
