- 2026-11-06: `/api/auth/logout` ends this browser's session only (`scope=local`). On `global`, one device signing out killed the account's other sessions, and their next request wiped their cookies. The heartbeat ticks every 5 minutes and refreshes once 50 have passed since the last refresh (`localStorage`), because `AppLayout` mounts per page and restarted a plain interval. An open visible tab left idle always signs out. Coming back past the limit signs out too, unless "Keep me signed in" is ticked and the company has not tightened the default 480 minutes (Hein, 2026-11-06), or the session is a Login-as, whose stored time belongs to an earlier visit. Only a real sign-in resets a time past the limit (`markSignedIn`: `/login`, `/accept-invite`, `AuthContext.login`), never `/login`'s mount or a renewal. `AppLayout` starts it once the company has loaded.
