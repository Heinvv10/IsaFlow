# Intercompany P4-D: receiver clearing of an allocation (contra-settle), 2026-11-23

Parent: docs/plans/2026-11-17-intercompany-mirror-posting.md (P4, rule 3 and 4). Stacked on P4-B (#1787, migration 1025) and P4-C (#1803, migration 1026); this slice is migration 1027. **This is the first P4 slice that posts to a ledger.** Rulings (orchestrator, 22:27, binding): a worker never self-approves and automation is never approval (the approver is the human who accepts the message on the receiver); `bankAllocationService` unchanged; loan clearing is a separate contra-settle journal through slice A's `settleDocumentByContra`; mismatch is state on the message (no new ledger object); reversals are out of P4 (#1772); a pre-cutover paired invoice or a locked period is REFUSED with the reason stored on the message, never rolled forward. D also closes the #1788 holes (direct-UPDATE `accepted`, open-amount cap, stale message).

## (a) What it does
A person with Approve permission on Customers & AR in the receiving company I opens an allocation message (C's inbox) and presses Accept. In ONE request transaction the service re-checks everything and posts exactly one contra settlement of I's customer invoice against I's loan account for the message's amount, then marks the message `accepted` pointing at the resulting customer payment. Nothing else is written: no bank leg, no VAT, no interest, no payer-side write (the payer's own clearing is E).

## (b) Every journal it posts (ONE per accepted message)
Journal J (posted by `settleDocumentByContra`, subledger `ar`, payment_method `contra`, migration 540):
- DR  I's loan account (the receiver side's `loan_account_id` on the connection) = amount
- CR  Accounts Receivable control (system account) = amount
- source: customer payment P (confirmed), applied to the issuer customer invoice for `amount`; `posting_agent` = `system:ic_allocation_clear` (labels the author; it is not an approver); `approved_by`/creator = the accepting person (creator == approver, required by the service until a decision record exists).
No other journal. Re-accepting is impossible (message final, one accepted message per target, UNIQUE below). A refused accept posts nothing (the service writes inside its savepoint and refuses before the first write).
Invoice effect (subledger, by the existing payment services): `amount_paid += amount`, status to `partially_paid` or `paid`. No change to any other invoice or customer balance.
Double-count guard (ruling): the receiver clears only against I's loan account, once per message. The message `amount` is capped at the invoice's OPEN amount at the moment of accept (refused, never clamped), so cash I already received and allocated against the same invoice through `bankAllocationService` leaves nothing to clear and the message is refused with `exceeds_open`. Tests: loan account movement equals the sum of accepted messages exactly; invoice open amount falls by exactly that; a bank-allocated invoice refuses; accepting the same message twice (concurrent requests) posts once.

## (c) Reversal path
Reversals are out of P4 (#1772); D adds none and documents the manual one: void the customer payment P with the existing receipt void (reverses J, restores the invoice's open amount). The message stays `accepted` (final) with a target payment that is voided; the guard keeps the link, P5 reconciliation reports "accepted message whose clearing was voided", and a re-clear is a new allocation message from the payer (it cannot be restored). D never reverses automatically (a payer-side unapply, void or journal reversal after the receiver accepted is a mismatch P5 lists; the receiver is not rolled back behind a person's back). Tests pin: voiding P leaves the message accepted and J reversed once.

## (d) Refusals (the reason is stored on the message; nothing posts)
Decided inside the accept transaction, in this order, after locking the message `FOR UPDATE`, then the invoice (lock order: message, invoice, then whatever the service locks; no other order exists):
Permanent (message becomes `rejected`, `reason` = a fixed sentence, `handled_via='user'`): `not_live` (C's definer says not live; the reason names it), `invoice_gone`, `invoice_not_open`, `currency` (not ZAR or differs), `pre_cutover_invoice` (invoice date before the connection cutover), `exceeds_open`, `not_loan`/`no_loan_account` (receiver side has no usable loan account).
Retryable (message becomes `failed`, `error` = a fixed sentence, stays in the open list for a person): `period_locked` (fiscal period or locked VAT period for the settlement date; checked BEFORE the first write by a pre-check, and a service refusal is mapped the same way), `contra_account_unusable` (loan account inactive or not postable at the moment), `busy` (lock bound hit).
Anything else (unexpected) propagates: the transaction rolls back and the message is untouched. Only `ContraGuardRefusal`/REFUSAL_CODES/MissingSystemAccountError count as refusals (slice A's rule). The stored sentence never contains raw SQL or ids.
Settlement date: the message's payment date (not today), refused as above if its period is locked: the person cannot silently post into a different period.

## (e) Guard (migration 1027, replaces the 1025 body; forward only, rollback header)
1. `allocation` joins the document-creating branch of the messages guard: accepting needs `target_doc_type = 'customer_payment'` and `intercompany_message_doc_exists('allocation','target','customer_payment', id, receiver)`; any target set on another transition is held to the same rule. Closes the direct-UPDATE `accepted` hole (#1788), so C's pin test flips on purpose.
2. `intercompany_message_doc_exists` (INVOKER) gains `allocation`/`target`: the customer payment exists in the receiver's company, `payment_method='contra'`, status confirmed, has a standing journal (odd chain length), and its amount equals the message payload amount (2 dp) and it applied to the invoice named by the payload's `issuerDocument.id`. A forged target (someone else's payment, wrong amount, wrong invoice, unconfirmed) is refused 23514.
3. One accepted message per target payment (existing rule) plus UNIQUE partial index `(to_company_id, source_doc_type, source_doc_id)` is already held by message identity; a second accepted message for the same allocation row cannot exist.
No new table, column or definer. `accepted` stays final. The auto-accept worker and queue keep their kind filter (tested): automation never accepts an allocation.

## (f) Code
`allocationClearService.ts` (`acceptAllocation({companyId, messageId, actor})`): requires a bound request transaction; reads the message (kind allocation, pending/failed, `to_company_id`), re-parses the payload, calls C's liveness definer, loads the invoice and side/connection (cutover, loan account), runs the refusals, calls `settleDocumentByContra` with `actor.userId == actor.approverUserId` = the session user (no worker id can reach it: the route takes the user from `withAuth`), then UPDATEs the message accepted with the target. Plus: FOR SHARE re-check of the contra account row and a fiscal-period / VAT-lock pre-check (the #1781 prerequisites that apply; the others, decision record and R-035 agent limits, do not: a human is present and the subledger is AR). Route `POST allocations/[id]/accept` (`withAuth(withErrorHandler(withCsrf(withWriteAccess(...))))`) that returns the posted journal and payment ids. UI: Accept button with a preview (DR loan account name, CR Accounts Receivable, amount, invoice, date) and a confirm; a handled row shows the payment and journal link. Files under 300 lines.

Unit: refusal mapping, sentence table, actor handling. Component: Accept preview and confirm, refusal display. Mutation checks: drop each refusal, the cap, the cutover check, the period pre-check, the target rule, the live check, the approver check, FOR UPDATE on the message.
Reviews: blind rounds, then an ADVERSARIAL LEDGER REVIEW and a policy-breaker (double clear, wrong tenant, forged target, approver spoof, stale-message clear, period shopping, concurrent accepts, loan-account tampering) before the queue.

## (h) Not in D
