# Intercompany P4-E: the payer clears a bill against the intercompany loan account, 2026-11-24

Parent: docs/plans/2026-11-17-intercompany-mirror-posting.md (rule 3: loan mode; P4 row). P4-B sends the payer's `allocation` message; P4-C shows it; P4-D lets the receiver clear its invoice. **E is the payer's own clearing: the human action that creates the payer-side journal the whole chain stands on** (DR Accounts Payable / CR the payer's loan account), through slice A's `settleDocumentByContra` (subledger `ap`). Rulings (orchestrator, binding): a worker never self-approves and automation is not approval (the approver is the human who presses it); `bankAllocationService` unchanged; loan clearing is a separate contra-settle journal; a mismatch is state, never a guess; reversals out of P4 (#1772); pre-cutover paired bill / locked period REFUSED, never rolled forward; **feature gate OFF** (see (g)).

## (a) What it does
A person with Approve on Suppliers & AP and the intercompany write role in the PAYING company P opens a supplier bill that is the AP side of an ACTIVE `intercompany_document_links` row (other side = the issuer's customer invoice) on an ACTIVE `loan` connection and presses "Clear against the intercompany loan". One request transaction re-checks everything and posts ONE contra settlement: **DR Accounts Payable control / CR P's loan account** for the amount, applied to the bill. The existing P4-B hook (after `processSupplierPayment`) then sends the `allocation` message by itself (`cashLegIsLoanAccount` true, because the journal credits the loan account). E writes no message itself and nothing on the receiver.

## (b) Every journal it posts (ONE per clearing) and its reversal path
Journal J: DR AP control (system account) / CR payer side's `loan_account_id`, amount, dated by the person (see (e)); source supplier payment (payment_method `contra`, migration 540: create, approve, process), applied to the bill; `posting_agent` `system:ic_loan_clear`; creator = approver = the session user. No bank leg, VAT, interest or receiver write.
Reversal path (none new; reversals are out of P4, #1772): the payer voids the supplier payment with the existing void/unapply (reverses J, reopens the bill). Effects: the allocation message (still pending) becomes not live (C's definer: `allocation_gone` / `payment_not_posted` / `journal_reversed`), so the receiver can no longer accept it; if the receiver ALREADY accepted (D), the receiver stays cleared and the payer is reopened: a mismatch that P5 reports (the reverse of #1833). E refuses nothing here and does not follow it.

## (c) Refusals (before any write; nothing to store on a message, so plain 4xx/refusal answers are safe because no write has happened)
Order, after locking the bill (`FOR UPDATE`), the link, connection and side `FOR SHARE`, the loan account `FOR SHARE`: feature off (`not_enabled`, 403); no session actor 'self' / impersonating (403); no intercompany write role or no Approve on Suppliers & AP (403, BEFORE posting: the allocation message is silently skipped for a user without the write role, so E must not post then); bill not found / deleted / not this company's (404); bill not approved / already paid / cancelled; no active link to a customer invoice (`no_link`); connection not active / not loan (`not_loan`); no cutover or payment date before the PAIR cutover (the same rule D uses, `intercompany_pair_cutovers`; the sender would never send); the payer's loan account fails `intercompany_is_loan_class` (system, bank, money or wrong class: D would refuse every message, `payer_loan_not_set_up`); amount not whole cents, <= 0, > the bill's open amount less pending draft payments; period locked / VAT-locked; contra account unusable. Slice A's `ContraGuardRefusal`/REFUSAL_CODES/MissingSystemAccountError are the only refusals it treats as such; anything else is a 5xx and rolls back.

## (d) Why this cannot double count (zero-double-count test)
After E on P (CR loan R) and D on I (DR loan R / CR AR R): P's loan credit equals I's loan debit; AP and AR each fall by R once. E clears the bill against the loan only up to its open amount, so cash already paid from the bank (payment applied) leaves nothing to clear; D's caps (by payer payment id/journal/bill) stop the receiver clearing more than the payer's loan credit. A second click on the same bill clears only what is still open (bill locked, open amount re-read).

## (e) Posting date and amount
Person-chosen `postingDate`: >= the bill date, >= the pair cutover, <= today, open fiscal period, not VAT-locked. Amount defaults to the open amount; a smaller whole-cent amount is a partial clearing (the allocation message carries that amount).

## (f) Code and UI
`payerLoanClearService.ts` (+ helpers < 300 lines): `clearBillAgainstLoan({companyId, billId, amount, postingDate, expected:{loanAccountId, invoiceLinkId, amount}, actor})`; route `POST /api/accounting/intercompany/loan-clearings`; supplier-bill detail action with preview (DR AP / CR loan account name, amount, date, partner invoice number) and confirm; the posted payment/journal ids shown. The expected-values check (like D) makes the person approve exactly what posts. Every query company-scoped.

## (g) Feature gate (the orchestrator's "keep the gate OFF")
No intercompany gate existed (P4-B/C/D shipped ungated). A dark feature flag `intercompany_loan_clearing` (feature_flags, is_global=false, enabled per company by a platform admin, `hasFeature`; the `budgets_three_way` precedent) is added by a separate small PR BEFORE E, gating D's accept service/route and the C inbox Accept button; E's route, service and button check it first. OFF means: no accept, no E action. (Outbox sending of allocation messages stays ungated: it is a no-effect outbox write; P5 reports read-only.)

## (h) Tests (first)
Integration as app_user, two tenants + a third: J is exactly one balanced journal with the specified legs and no other journal; bill open amount and loan balance move by exactly amount; the allocation message is sent with `cashLegIsLoanAccount` true and the receiver (D) can clear it: both loan balances equal, AP and AR each cleared once (the zero-double-count test); second click refused/only remainder; concurrent clicks post once; each refusal posts nothing; no write role / no Approve refused BEFORE posting; flag off refused; unlinked bill, cash connection, pre-cutover, locked period, bad loan account, bank-paid bill refused; void of the payment reverses J once and the pending message turns not live; expected-values mismatch 409; cross-tenant 404. Unit for refusals/sentences; component for the preview and confirm. Mutation checks on every refusal, the lock, the flag and the approver checks. Adversarial ledger review plus policy-breaker before the queue.
