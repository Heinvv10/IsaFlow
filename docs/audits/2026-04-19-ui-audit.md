## Practice → Add Client (the reported bug) — could not reproduce

What I tested:
1. Direct nav to `/accounting/practice/clients/new` → form renders correctly.
2. Click Practice tab → flyout opens → click "Add a Client" → lands on `/accounting/practice/clients/new` (form renders).
3. Click "Add Client" CTA on the practice clients list → same destination.

All three flows stay inside the app. No external/marketing redirect, no login redirect, no out-of-tenant jump.

**One suspicious observation worth flagging:** on my first visit to `/accounting/practice/clients` (immediately after `/accounting/practice/clients/new`), the URL showed `/accounting/practice/clients` but the page body briefly rendered "Recurring Transactions" content. Subsequent loads were clean. This looks like a Next.js client-side router/hydration race where the previous page's body remains painted while the new pathname is committed. If you saw the redirect happen as part of clicking around quickly, this is the most likely culprit — it's transient and not reproducible deterministically.

If the bug is real and persistent, please grab a Sentry trace ID or a browser network log of the moment it happens and reopen — I can chase a deterministic repro from there.
