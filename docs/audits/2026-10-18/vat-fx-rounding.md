## PLAUSIBLE (not proven)

- `getVATReturn`'s generic-`tax` sweep (V9b) hurts only a company whose PAYE or income-tax payable account carries subtype `tax` on a liability or asset. Dev has none.
- `exchangeRate:"18.5"` sent as a string is silently replaced by 1 (`shared/documentCurrency.ts:105`). This matters only once `fx_documents_enabled` is switched on, and nothing turns it on today. Likewise, foreign invoices would post document-currency figures as rands if the switch were enabled before functional posting lands.
- `bankQifParser.ts:93` and `bankPdfParserStandardBank.ts:62` strip commas the same way as M3.
- Mileage `Number((km*rate).toFixed(2))` (`expenseClaimService.ts:170`, `payrollBatchService.ts:324`) can drop half a cent.
- The `amount_paid >= total − 0.01 → paid` rule can leave a permanent 0.01 on AR/AP.
- `receiptToJournalService.ts:207`: when no date is extracted, the fallback `new Date().toISOString()` dates the receipt a day early before 02:00 SAST.

## What was retracted during the audit

Running `deriveVatBoxes` alone suggested that a mixed standard + zero-rated **input** journal fails the 15% check. The real pipeline (`deriveVatBoxesWithLateClaims` → `applyCodedBaseRule` + `perJournalInputOverclaim`) handles it: re-run, inputVariance 0 and ties true. It is not reported. Only the output side (V11) remains.
