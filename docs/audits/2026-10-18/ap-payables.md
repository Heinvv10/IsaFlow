## PLAUSIBLE (not verified)

- Reversing a bill's approval journal directly (`reverse_journal_entry`) leaves the bill approved and open. Post-to-GL then answers `alreadyPosted` without checking `reversed_at`, so the subledger holds a bill the GL doesn't. A later supplier CN against it would credit legs the GL no longer carries.
- Approve reuses a legacy draft's existing journal without checking whether that journal was reversed.
- A draft supplier CN linked to a bill that is later cancelled: the cancel plan doesn't refuse it; behaviour on approval was not traced.
- Bill and supplier-CN VAT legs are always `vatType:'standard'`, so capital-goods input VAT may miss its VAT201 box (related to K-192).
- Voiding an on-account payment after a refund was measured against it drives the on-account credit negative.
- When Netcash returns a 200 with a non-JSON body, the send is recorded `failed`, which invites a resubmit (makes AP-01 worse).
- `reports/purchases-by-item.ts` filters `stock_items.company_id` but not `po.company_id`.
- An MCP credit-note subtotal with more than 2 decimals can make subtotal + tax ≠ total by 1c, so approval fails.
- `capture/[id]/post` fires `resolvePendingAlertForInvoice` without awaiting it on the request transaction; an error there would poison the transaction after the bill insert.
