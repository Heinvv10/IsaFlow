# Practice (PRC) — audit

Scope: practice management, time, WIP, fee calculation, client portals. Company: ISAFlow Demo (dev `isaflow_dev`), user reporting-qa. Code: `.worktrees/uiux-audit` @ 66e776d81.
Shots: `$AUD/shots/practice/` (bulk captures), `$AUD/shots/practice-flows/` (interactive runs; scripts `$AUD/scripts/practice-flows.mjs`, `practice-flows2.mjs`).

## Verdict
Practice management covers the basic parts: clients, jobs (list and kanban), tasks, a day-based time form, approvals, a WIP board, rates and six reports. The pieces are not connected into a working practice. The owner's ask was "practice management and fee calculations". **No fee calculation or engagement-pricing tool exists anywhere, in the UI or the code.** Billing model, fixed fee and retainer are labels only; no billing code reads them. The flow from time to WIP to invoice cannot be completed from the UI: a practice client can never be linked to an AR customer, so WIP can never be invoiced (P0). Time cannot be attached to a job. A fresh firm cannot log an hour until it adds work items by hand. There is no single place where a partner sees clients × jobs × deadlines × WIP × profitability. `/accounting/practice` itself returns 404. "Practice clients" have no link to the client companies whose books live in IsaFlow (Portfolio). This is the biggest structural gap.

## Top 5 recommendations for this area
1. **Make the billing chain completable**: link a practice client to an AR customer (and to its IsaFlow company) at creation and in an Edit screen. Fix "View invoice". (PRC-01, 05, 06, 10)
2. **Build the fee engine the owner asked for**: service packages × volume drivers from the linked books → suggested fee, then engagement letter, recurring or fixed-fee invoice, then fee vs effort margin. Until then, the billing-model and fixed-fee fields are decoration. (PRC-03, 27, 12)
3. **One Practice home and a client 360 page**: clients × jobs × deadlines × WIP × margin, exceptions first, with one-click Bill / Chase / Open books. (PRC-04, 26)
4. **Make time capture usable on day one and in one place**: seed work items (or make them optional), add Job to the entry, add a timer and a practice-aware week grid, and retire the two legacy time screens. (PRC-02, 08, 09, 28)
5. **Add a compliance calendar per client**: SARS/CIPC deadlines turned into auto-tasks, and rename or rebuild the mislabelled Compliance and Pipeline reports. (PRC-13, 11)
