# Purchases / AP — audit

Code: `.worktrees/uiux-audit` @ 66e776d81. App: localhost:3200, company ISAFlow Demo, user reporting-qa.
Shots: `$AUD/shots/purchases/` (`*.png`/`*.json` per route, `*.m.png` mobile, `flow/` interactive).
Scripts: `$AUD/scripts/purchases-{dropdowns,bill-flow,po-flow,capture-flow}.mjs`.
Drafts I created (Demo only): supplier **UXAUDIT Flow Supplier** (`9ca50fc2-48cd-4661-b581-715bc62313bc`) and draft bill **SIV0001004**, ref `UXAUDIT-BILL-1` (`28b71f7a-74ba-4bab-b11d-dfe9c59d38d1`). Nothing was approved, posted, paid or sent.

## Verdict
The Supplier Invoices list is the most modern screen in this area: URL-backed filters, status tabs with counts, "signals" chips, bulk bar and breadcrumbs. Around it, AP data paths are broken in ways that give wrong numbers or block basic work:
- Every "new document" form offers only the first 25 suppliers.
- The bill form shows VAT that does not match its own VAT rate.
- Allocate Payments lists fully allocated payments as unallocated, with a false R 483k total.
- The supplier statement disagrees with the aging for the same supplier.
- Suppliers cannot be edited after they are created.

The capture/AI flow looks promising but hides what makes it AI: no preview, no confidence score, no suggested coding. Several features have no way in from the menus (statement reconciliation, expectations, the second PO system).

## Top 5 recommendations for this area
1. **Fix the AP data paths that show wrong numbers or block work**: supplier pickers capped at 25 (PUR-01), bill VAT mismatch (PUR-02), fake unallocated payments (PUR-03), statement vs aging (PUR-04), supplier not editable (PUR-07). Each is small and all of them undermine trust.
2. **One PO system, and a working PO → GRN → Bill chain**: retire `supplier_purchase_orders` (PUR-05/06). "Create GRN" should prefill from the PO, and the PO page should show its GRNs and bills with 3-way match status (PUR-11).
3. **Make capture a real AI review queue**: image next to editable fields, confidence, suggested supplier/GL/VAT from rules, duplicate warning, approve-and-next, rules learned from corrections. Merge `/capture`, `/capture/inbox` and `/document-capture` into one hub (PUR-08/09/14/15/32).
4. **Supplier 360° plus one AP to-do surface**: balance, aging, open bills, statement, POs and actions on the supplier page (PUR-19). An "AP today" inbox covering review, approve, pay-run and statement mismatches, fed by the existing signals, and one approvals queue that includes draft bills (PUR-18/31/33).
5. **Give month-end AP a route in and a pay-run wizard**: surface Statement Reconciliation and Expected/Missing invoices in the menu and delete the dead nav file (PUR-17). Replace the row-by-row batch form with a due-date pay run that checks bank details (PUR-20).
