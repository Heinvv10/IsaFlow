# Sales / AR — audit

Paths: `S=$AUD/shots/sales`, `F=$AUD/shots/sales/flows`, code under `.worktrees/uiux-audit` (66e776d81). Company: ISAFlow Demo. Session: reporting-qa. No records created, sent or submitted (all dialogs dismissed; no POST other than analytics tracking).

## Verdict
All 33 routes load with no failed API calls. The area is still a set of form-first silos. The customer page is a read-only business card: it shows no balance and has no edit, new-invoice or statement actions. No customer edit screen exists anywhere in the UI. Five views of "what the customer owes" contradict each other. The invoice list says "Overdue 0 – All on time" while R10.8M is 90+ days overdue.
Quote → sales order → invoice conversion works, but only as a one-shot: confirmed sales orders cannot be reopened from the list, delivery notes cannot become invoices, and credit notes cannot be raised against an invoice. Several newer pages (dunning, disputes, receipt-match) are light-theme islands with invisible H1s in dark mode, and three pages drop out of the Sales nav.
What works: invoice detail has a breadcrumb, a context-aware Back, prev/next, a GL link and grouped actions. Record Payment from an invoice pre-fills. The Debtors Manager / aging grid is solid.

## Top 5 recommendations for this area
1. **One AR truth + explainable balances** (SAL-02/03/04/29). Every screen should read the same credit-adjusted balance and show an "Unapplied" bucket. Remove the page-scoped KPIs.
2. **A real customer 360 with edit** (SAL-01/06). Editable profile, balance/aging header, open items with inline Receive/Credit/Remind, statement tab, and a timeline. This should be where invoices, payments and statements start from (with `?clientId` prefill everywhere).
3. **A linked document chain** (SAL-05/07/08). Detail pages for quotes; every SO row openable. Quote → SO → DN → Invoice → Receipt/CN shown as a clickable chain, with one "Next step" button. Credit notes raised from an invoice with its lines.
4. **A Collections inbox replacing 7 pages** (SAL-17/18/27). Exceptions-first queue that merges aging, dunning, disputes, promises, unapplied cash and statements, with AI-drafted reminders and confident auto-match suggestions.
5. **Shell consistency pass** (SAL-11/13/14/16/19). Fix nav matchers and the orphan, port light-only pages to theme tokens, fix modal z-index, standardise dates/money to en-ZA, and make action bars collapse on mobile.
