# Ledger (core accounting, month-end/year-end, assets, loans, migration) — audit

Paths: `S=$AUD/shots/ledger`, `F=$S/flow`, repo = `.worktrees/uiux-audit` @ 66e776d81. Company: ISAFlow Demo (dev DB). Desktop 1440, mobile 390.

## Verdict
The ledger area loads cleanly (55 routes, 0 page errors, one 404) and some parts are good: the TB→account-detail drill keeps its filters through `return_to`, the Evidence modal and Close Agent are the right ideas, and the asset detail page with its IAS16/tax/deferred-tax schedule is solid.
But **the numbers don't tie between screens**, which is what an accountant checks first:
- TB and account detail disagree for P&L accounts.
- There are three different accumulated-depreciation figures.
- Asset cost and loans are missing from the GL.
- AR in the subledger is R10.8m, but the TB shows R154k.
- The depreciation screen says there are no assets.

The drill chain also stops at the account ledger. Rows can't be clicked, a JE never links to its source document, and CoA rows go nowhere. Month-end is split across 5 unconnected concepts: the workbench, Close Agent, the fiscal-periods checklist (which only appears in the tab variant), the Loans period lock and Year-End. It is not one guided close.

## Top 5 recommendations for this area
1. **Make every number tie and say so.** Fix the P&L opening basis in account detail and the GL report (LED-01). Add subledger↔control reconciliations for AR, AP, FAR and Loans, with a visible "ties ✓/✗" on the TB, workbench and module pages (LED-03/04/05/14).
2. **Complete the drill chain in both directions:** TB → account → line → JE → source document (invoice/bill/payment/asset/loan), and back via `return_to` at each hop. Also CoA → ledger (LED-07/08/18).
3. **Make one guided month-end per period:** merge the workbench, Close Agent, readiness checklist, loans lock and Year-End into one checklist and exceptions inbox that links to filtered lists and gates the close (LED-12/13/27/31).
4. **Rebuild journal entry:** one paged and searchable JE list; a draft/post model that matches its labels, with Edit/Delete on drafts; a searchable account picker, attachments, templates and AI-suggested lines (LED-09/10/33).
5. **Fix the silent breakages:** depreciation list (LED-02), wrong account on account-transactions (LED-06), blank migration validate (LED-17), unclosable Evidence modal (LED-11). Standardise en-ZA dates and money plus breadcrumbs across the area (LED-22/25).
