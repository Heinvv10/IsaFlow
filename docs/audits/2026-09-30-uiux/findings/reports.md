# Reports, AFS, management packs, group — audit

Company: ISAFlow Demo (`00000000-…0001`), dev DB, 2026-09-30. Shots: `$AUD/shots/reports/` (per-route PNG+JSON), flows in `$AUD/shots/reports/flow/`, downloads in `$AUD/downloads-reports/`. Code refs are to `.worktrees/uiux-audit` (66e776d81).

## Verdict
The core statements (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, account drill-down) are good: comparatives, variance bars, an "assumptions" banner that explains classification risk, and a drill from the BS to the account ledger that keeps the period and has a working "Back to Balance Sheet". Around that core, the area is not trustworthy yet. **Six surfaces give six different cash figures for the same date.** The Three-Way Forecast drops minus signs, so losses display as profits. Financial Analysis crashes. The AI Cash Flow Forecast returns a 500. Four of nine present-mode slides say "no presentation renderer yet", and the management pack prints `null`, `[object Object]` and a developer note. About 20 reports default to a start date one day early (a UTC shift), and VAT201 defaults to 31 Jul – 30 Aug. There are two report hubs, four "pack" systems, three forecasts, two consolidation modules and several exact duplicate reports. Transactional reports are dead ends: there are no links to the invoice, journal or customer behind a row, and 6,581 rows render with no paging. It reads as a report catalogue, not an AI-driven reporting product. No "explain this number" surface exists in any report (grep of `pages/accounting/reports`, `src/components/reports` found none).

## Top 5 recommendations for this area
1. **One cash truth (RPT-01/02/09).** Put every cash figure (Cash Flow, Mgmt Accounts, Packs, Forecasts, Cash Movement, AFS) on one engine that reconciles to GL cash, and show the reconciliation line on each report. As things stand an accountant can't hand any pack to a client.
2. **Fix the wrong-number and crash bugs this week (RPT-03/04/05/06/07/08).** These are the Math.abs signs, the null ratio crash, the dead AI forecast, the UTC off-by-one defaults (VAT201 included), the `null`/`[object Object]` in packs, and the BvA -100%/mixed total. Most are one-line fixes.
3. **One report shell and one hub (RPT-13/14/23).** Generate hub and nav from a registry, add search, recents and favourites, and one period picker with SA presets. Give every report the same Export XLSX/CSV/PDF, Print, Present and breadcrumb, and keep one back target.
4. **Make reports navigable (RPT-16/17/31).** Link every row to its source document with `return_to`. Show summary first with expandable, paged detail. Add "Explain this number" on statement cells.
5. **Collapse the duplicates (RPT-12/15/21).** Merge the four pack systems into one client-pack flow. Remove the exact-duplicate customer and supplier reports, pick one of the three forecasts and one of the two consolidation modules, and separate the audit trail from the journal listing.
