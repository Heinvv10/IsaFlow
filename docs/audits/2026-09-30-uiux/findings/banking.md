# Banking — audit

Code: `.worktrees/uiux-audit` @ 66e776d81. App: localhost:3200 (isaflow_dev). Sessions: ISAFlow Demo (`sessions/banking.json`), Brightsphere (`sessions/banking-brit.json`).
Shots: `$AUD/shots/banking/` (static captures) and `$AUD/shots/banking/flow/` (interactive). Scripts: `$AUD/scripts/banking-tx.mjs`, `$AUD/scripts/banking-nav.mjs`.
Nothing was accepted, allocated, excluded, reconciled, sent or saved. Every modal was opened and then cancelled. One side effect happens without any click: the bank-transactions page POSTs `/api/accounting/bank-relink-transfers` on every load (see BNK-27).

## Verdict
The daily coding grid is capable. It has AI/rule suggestions, split, find-and-match, create-rule, attachments, ask-client, bulk edit and a keyboard row stream. But it is a dense Sage-style grid with 7 unlabelled icons per row, and suggestion confidence is only a 6px dot. The bigger problem is trust: several headline numbers in Banking are wrong or mislabelled.
- The "GL Balance" is the sum of the statement lines, not the ledger.
- "Unallocated" counts reconciled rows.
- The Cashbook ignores its own date filter and starts the running balance at zero.
- The reconciliation screen silently caps at 100 lines: it shows "100 unmatched" when there are 8 089.
- The PDF-import account picker has blank labels.
Navigation is decent within Banking (Back links exist on sub-pages). But no page has a breadcrumb, and 6 banking routes highlight **Dashboard** in the top nav. Mobile coding is not usable.

## Top 5 recommendations for this area
1. **Fix the numbers first:** BNK-01 (Cashbook), BNK-02 (100-line reconciliation cap), BNK-03 ("GL Balance" is not the GL), BNK-04 (Unallocated counts reconciled rows). An accountant who sees one wrong balance stops trusting the whole module.
2. **Exceptions-first coding queue** (BNK-30/16): accept all high-confidence suggestions in one action with undo, show confidence and reason visibly, and leave only low-confidence and unknown lines for manual work.
3. **One statement-vs-ledger truth per account** (BNK-03/12/33/34): show statement closing, ledger balance and the gap on the account card, the coding page and the reconciliation, with one click from the gap to the lines causing it.
4. **Traceability and navigable state** (BNK-06/07/31): every reviewed line links to its journal or document, tab and filters live in the URL, and a detail drawer replaces the 7 icon modals.
5. **Close the client-query loop and fix navigation** (BNK-32/08/13/29): batch "ask client" with replies written back onto the lines; one import entry point; a flat Banking menu with queue counts; correct Banking highlighting on all 6 misrouted pages.
