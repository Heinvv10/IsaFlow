## Summary: Competitive Strengths & Weaknesses

### Strengths
1. **Strong SA bank feed integration** -- direct feeds with all major SA banks
2. **SARS compliance** -- VAT201 electronic submission, payroll tax compliance
3. **Affordable entry price** -- R240/month for Start plan
4. **Customer portal** -- Online invoice viewing, quote acceptance, payments
5. **Mobile app** -- Functional iOS/Android app for on-the-go invoicing
6. **Recurring invoices** -- Automated scheduling with email delivery
7. **CaseWare integration** -- Important for SA accountants
8. **Netcash batch payments** -- Automated supplier payment processing
9. **Established brand** -- Trusted by SA accountants, large partner network

### Weaknesses
1. **No fixed asset management** -- Critical gap for SA businesses
2. **No purchase orders** -- Cannot do procurement workflow
3. **No AI features** -- All AI investment goes to Sage Intacct
4. **No custom reports** -- Cannot build bespoke report layouts
5. **No multi-entity/consolidation** -- Each company is isolated
6. **No OCR/document capture** -- Manual data entry required
7. **No approval workflows** -- For invoices, bills, or payments
8. **No tracking categories** -- Cannot tag transactions to departments/projects at GL level
9. **Basic inventory** -- No multi-warehouse, serial, batch, or assembly
10. **No WIP tracking** -- Inadequate for professional services firms
11. **Payroll is separate** -- Additional cost, not integrated real-time
12. **Feature gating via add-ons** -- Multi-currency, time tracking, inventory all cost extra
13. **No report scheduling** -- Cannot automate report distribution
14. **Limited budgeting** -- Single version, no forecasting
15. **Small app marketplace** -- Fewer integrations than Xero/QuickBooks
