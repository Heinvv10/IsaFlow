# MCP goods received notes (section 3)

Status: design, 2026-11-20. Builds on the PO bill links (#1627) and PO maintenance (#1665) work, both on master.

## Tools

| Tool | Kind | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| `create_goods_received` | write | write:invoices |
| `cancel_goods_received` | write | write:invoices |
| `list_goods_received` | read | read:invoices |
| `get_goods_received` | read | read:invoices |

Both writes are free flows (`purchaseOrderFlow.ts`), like the PO tools. No new scope, so no connector re-consent.

`create_goods_received(po_id, received_date, received_by?, notes?, lines[{po_item_id, quantity_received, quantity_rejected?, notes?}], external_system?, external_ref?, idempotency_key, dry_run?)`

- It runs `createGoodsReceived` (the service the app's POST uses), so the rules are the same: the PO must be `approved` or `partially_received`; `quantity_received` must not exceed the line's ordered quantity less what was already received (rejected goods do not count against it); every line must belong to the PO; and no PO line may appear twice. The PO status is then recomputed to `partially_received` or `received`.
- Numbering stays the existing `GRN-00001` sequence, under number lock 620.
- `external_system` + `external_ref` are optional. When given, they are unique per company among GRNs that are not cancelled. A repeat names the existing GRN in a refusal (`duplicate_external_ref`) and writes nothing. FibreFlow site deliveries send `external_system: "fibreflow"`.
- Refusals are typed and per line (`po_not_receivable`, `over_receipt` with the line, outstanding and asked quantities, `line_not_on_po`, `duplicate_line`, `duplicate_external_ref`, `not_found`). They are all-or-nothing: if any line is refused, nothing is written.
- `dry_run` runs the real service in a rolled-back savepoint and reports `grn_number` as "next". It sets `wroteNothing` and does not consume the idempotency key.

`cancel_goods_received(grn_id, reason, idempotency_key, dry_run?)`

- It sets the GRN to `cancelled` (with `cancelled_at`, `cancelled_by` and `cancel_reason`), subtracts each item's `quantity_received` from its `po_items.quantity_received`, and recomputes the PO status (`received` → `partially_received` / `approved`). These three happen in one SECURITY DEFINER function, `goods_received_note_cancel` (see Database rules); the service keeps the guards that name things, the locks and the audit row.
- It refuses when:
  - the GRN is already cancelled (`already_cancelled`);
  - the PO is `cancelled` (`po_not_receivable`). A cancelled PO carries only cancelled notes (`cancel_purchase_order` and `set_purchase_order_status` refuse a PO with a live one), so a live note there was put by hand, and no tool mends it: the refusal says so and points to IsaFlow support rather than to a status change that does not exist;
  - an item of the note names a PO line that is not on the note's PO (`items_not_on_po`, naming the lines). Only a legacy row written before `grn_items_po_line_guard` can do that; the reversal takes quantities off the note's PO's lines only, so it would report success and leave that line's received quantity in place. The service decides it on its own read before calling the function (so it waits on no note lock), and the function re-decides it for a direct caller. Nothing is written;
  - taking the note off would leave a PO line with less received quantity than the PO's other live notes still account for (`receipts_disagree`, naming the lines with `line_received`, `note_received` and `other_notes_received`; the line's total was not written with the notes). This also covers a line that would fall below zero. Nothing is written;
  - a bill that is neither deleted nor cancelled (a NULL status counts as live: `IS DISTINCT FROM 'cancelled'`) carries this GRN in `supplier_invoices.grn_id` (`grn_on_bill`, naming the bills). The exit is to cancel or delete those bills (a paid bill needs its payment reversed before it can be cancelled), then cancel the note. Nothing edits a bill's `grn_id`.
- A note on a **closed** PO cancels. The PO stays closed (closing is a decision, and no tool reopens a PO that has receipts), the quantities come back as usual, and the answer carries `receipt_status`: what the receipts now give (`received` / `partially_received` / `approved`). The records of the status the PO was closed from follow it, so a later reopen lands right: `purchase_orders.closed_from_status` (1007, set when closed by hand) and `purchase_order_bill_links.closed_po_from_status` (1006, set when a link closed it; a closing record is moved, not rewritten, so it is cleared and set again).
- Billed quantity above received quantity is allowed after a cancel, as it already is when a bill arrives before the goods do.
- Managers and above may cancel (the write fence); `goods_received_note_cancel` decides it again for a direct caller and answers `role_not_allowed` to any other member, before any lock (merge-gate finding 1 of round 2). The commitment is unchanged, because `approved`, `partially_received` and `received` all count as committed.
- A PO whose notes are all cancelled has no receipt: `cancel_purchase_order` and `update_purchase_order` (header) work again (review M2). A cancelled note keeps its items, which reference the PO's lines, so replacing those lines and setting an imported PO back to draft (where the PUT replaces lines and the DELETE removes the PO) stay refused, naming the cancelled note.

`list_goods_received(po_id?, supplier_id?, date_from?, date_to?, external_ref?, status?, limit, offset)` returns notes with their PO number, supplier, line count and document count: 25 a page by default, 30 at most, with `next_offset`, and a compact projection (received_by, supplier name, notes and cancel reason cut at 80 characters), so the widest page stays under the read limit (Codex round 3, finding 5). `get_goods_received(grn_id, items_offset?, items_limit?)` returns the note, a page of its items (with the PO line description, ordered and received quantities; at most 40, with `items_total`, `items_truncated` and `items_next_offset`), its attachments (entity types `goods_received` and the legacy `goods_receipt_note`; the list's document count counts both), and the bills that carry its `grn_id`. The caps and text limits keep the widest note (100 items, every text at its limit in three-byte characters, documents and bills at their caps) under the 90,000-byte read limit on every page. An item whose PO line is on another PO (a legacy row) is listed with `on_purchase_order: false` and the line's metadata null, so `items_total` counts what the cancel's `items_not_on_po` refuses. Cancelled notes are listed with `status: cancelled`.

## GL

A GRN does not post to the general ledger. It records quantities only: there is no GRNI accrual and no stock movement journal. The ledger moves when the bill is approved. Both tools' descriptions say so, and an integration test asserts that create and cancel leave `gl_journal_entries` and `gl_journal_lines` unchanged.

## A bill's GRN (review M1)

`supplier_invoices.grn_id` has NO foreign key (migration 201 created it without one; 246b's `ADD COLUMN ... REFERENCES` was skipped because the column existed), so the database ties nothing. The writers are `createSupplierInvoice` (the REST create; MCP, bulk, import, recurring and capture creates never set it) and the MCP draft edit `update_supplier_invoice` (its `grn_id`: null takes the note off, an id names another), plus the undo of a soft-deleted bill, which makes a bill live again. All go through `ap/billGoodsReceivedLink.ts`:

- the create refuses a malformed id, a note that is not this company's (or does not exist) and a cancelled note (`SUPPLIER_INVOICE_GRN_REFUSED`, 400; the REST route checks ahead of its number reservation too);
- the note must agree with the bill (merge-gate finding 1, decided for Hein): its PO is a PO of the bill's supplier, and when the bill names POs (its header `purchase_order_id`, the POs its lines' `po_item_id`s belong to) the note's PO is one of them. A bill for supplier B naming a live note on supplier A's PO in the same company is refused, on the create, the REST pre-check and the undo alike (same refusal);
- the undo refuses a bill whose note (of this company) was cancelled while the bill was deleted, or does not agree with it as above (the POs it names include its link rows);
- the draft edit judges the note the bill will carry whenever it changes the supplier or `grn_id` (merge-gate finding 3 of round 2): the new supplier, and the POs the bill names (header, lines, link rows; the edit changes none of them). A supplier change the note would no longer agree with is refused, naming the exit: `grn_id: null`, or a live note that agrees, in the same edit. It holds the note after the links refresh (bill -> PO -> note), and when the note or the supplier changes (the tolerances are the supplier's coding rules) it re-judges the bill's stored three-way match in the same transaction (`resetMatchState`: `performThreeWayMatch` when the bill names a PO, else unmatched; round 3, finding 1);
- the PO link tools keep the note on a PO the bill names (posted bills, whose note cannot be edited; `note_po_not_named`, typed, dry run too): `link_bills_to_purchase_order` refuses each new row of a bill carrying a note whose PO the bill would not name after the call (its header, lines, links and the call's rows), the exit being to include the note's PO for that bill in the same call; `unlink_bill_from_purchase_order` refuses unlinking the note's PO while the bill still names another, the exit being to unlink those first or cancel the bill. Unlinking the last PO a bill names stays allowed (the supplier still agrees);
- all hold the note `FOR SHARE` (`goods_received_note_hold`) and judge it under that lock. The cancel takes the note `FOR UPDATE` and then looks for live bills, so exactly one wins: never a cancelled note with a live bill.

Decided: a typed check under a row lock, not a foreign key. Adding the FK means validating whatever production holds (dangling ids are possible after 201) and still would not see the note's status.

## Lock order

Create takes number lock 620, then the PO `FOR UPDATE`, then `po_items FOR UPDATE` (the app's POST order). Cancel takes NO number lock (merge-gate finding 5): the PO `FOR UPDATE`, then `po_items FOR UPDATE`, then the note `FOR UPDATE` inside the function, every wait under one 5 s `withRowLockBudget` deadline (`PURCHASE_ORDER_LOCK_TIMEOUT` for the PO rows, `ROW_LOCK_TIMEOUT` for the note; typed, retryable, nothing written). Holding 620 while blocked on a PO row stalled every create of the company. The cancel assigns no number, and it only ever frees an external reference (live -> cancelled): a create that reads the note as live while its cancel is uncommitted refuses `duplicate_external_ref` (retry once it commits), and the partial unique index `goods_received_notes_external_ref_uq` is behind that check, so a create racing a cancel never leaves two live notes with one reference (`mcp-goods-received-cancel-locks.int.test.ts`, deterministic and raced). A bill takes the note LAST: the create after every PO row it locks (`assertBillPoLinks`, `recordBillLinks`), the undo after the bill row. Taking it before `recordBillLinks` deadlocked against a cancel when the bill named the note's PO (reproduced in `mcp-goods-received-bills.int.test.ts`); the ranks are bill < PO < note for everyone, and 620 is held only by creates, which take it first, so no cycle (census in `mcp-po-bill-links.md`).

## Migration

- Add `cancelled` to `goods_received_notes_status_check`.
- Add the columns `cancelled_at`, `cancelled_by`, `cancel_reason`, `external_system` and `external_ref`.
- A CHECK keeps the cancel columns consistent: they are set if and only if the status is `cancelled`, and a reason is required.
- A partial unique index on `(company_id, external_system, external_ref)` covers non-cancelled notes that have an `external_ref`.
- A trigger refuses moving a note out of `cancelled`, refuses editing a cancelled note's items, and refuses inserting items into a cancelled note. Another (`goods_received_notes_insert_guard`, BEFORE INSERT, ALWAYS) refuses inserting a note that is already cancelled or carries any `cancelled_*` column, so only the definer's UPDATE cancels (Codex round 3, finding 3).
- A trigger (`grn_items_same_transaction_guard`) refuses inserting an item onto a note that was not created in the same transaction (the note's `created_xact`, which its BEFORE INSERT trigger always sets to `pg_current_xact_id()`, the top-level transaction id, is not the current one; not `created_at`, which the caller can supply, and not `xmin`, which a savepoint changes; round 5, finding 2). `created_at` is left as it is: informational. Create writes a note, its items and the PO lines' `quantity_received` together; an item added later would leave that total disagreeing with the notes.
- Two more triggers keep a note on one company and one PO: `goods_received_notes_po_guard` (the PO named is a PO of the note's company) and `grn_items_po_line_guard` (an item names a line of its note's PO). A foreign key checks neither.
- `idx_supplier_invoices_grn` on `supplier_invoices (company_id, grn_id) WHERE grn_id IS NOT NULL`: the cancel's check for a live bill scans bills under the PO and note locks.

## Database rules (review M3, L1)

- **Only the cancel changes a note.** No app path UPDATEs or DELETEs `goods_received_notes` or `grn_items` except the cancel, and a trigger keyed on the caller's role can be steered, so app_user loses UPDATE and DELETE on both (a privilege holds). The cancel's state change, quantity reversal and PO recompute run in `goods_received_note_cancel(note, reason)`: SECURITY DEFINER, owner `migration_admin`, `search_path = pg_catalog, public, pg_temp`, EXECUTE for app_user / app_service only, registered in `app_definer_reviews` (930). Inside it: the caller must be a member (`company_users`, `auth.uid()`) of the session company (`app.current_company_id()`), else `not_found`; it takes the create's locks itself and re-decides `already_cancelled`, `po_not_receivable` and `grn_on_bill` under the note lock, answering them as an outcome (nothing written) rather than raising. `cancelled_by` is `auth.uid()`.
- **The hold.** A row lock needs UPDATE privilege, so a bill cannot take `FOR SHARE` on a note itself any more: `goods_received_note_hold(note)` (same definer rules; reads one row of the session company for a member, writes nothing) takes it and returns the status.
- **Legacy document type.** `procurement_documents`' policy admits entity_type `goods_receipt_note` through the same parent check as `goods_received` (a note of the caller's companies to read, of the session company to write): the bill's listing reads a note's documents of both types, and the documents DELETE (`is_active = false`) must pass WITH CHECK on such a row. No app path inserts that type (`VALID_ENTITY_TYPES` omits it). 497 had left it invisible to app_user.
- **grn_items row security.** `grn_items` has no `company_id`; it gets row security (FORCE, like its note) with a policy through the note: visible when the note is (the note's own policy), written only onto a note of the session company (WITH CHECK). Create, cancel, get / list, the three-way match and the item-movement report read through it unchanged.

## Attachments

The app's goods received list (`/accounting/goods-received`) shows each note's status as a pill, and a cancelled note's reason under it.

A bill's documents include its note's and its POs' documents, resolved at READ time (merge-gate finding 4). Every linked document, an older `document_links` row's included, is listed only when its own parent (`entity_type` + `entity_id`) is a row of the selected company (round 2, finding 2); the listing first checks the id is a row of the REQUESTED type of that company (round 3, finding 2). An older automatic row onto a bill (`po_invoice_link` / `grn_invoice_link`) is shown only while its document's parent is the bill's current note or a PO it names now (header, lines, links), manual rows always (round 3, finding 4); a note's listing reads rows linked under the legacy alias `goods_receipt_note` too (round 3, finding 6). The mirror (review of round 3, M): a note's or a PO's linked documents are the documents of the live bills of the company that name it NOW (a note: their `grn_id`; a PO: header, lines, links), resolved at read time, and an older automatic row onto the note or PO shows only while its bill still names it. Decided: read time, not more link writes, as for the bill side: after a draft moves from note N1 to N2 the bill's scan leaves N1 and shows on N2 without a new row; after an unlink the PO no longer lists it. The documents POST and DELETE check the entity's own type (`verifyEntityOwnershipTyped`; a legacy `goods_receipt_note` document is checked as a note): `listEntityDocuments` (the documents GET and `list_documents`) adds, for a supplier invoice, the active documents filed on its `grn_id` (entity types `goods_received` and the legacy `goods_receipt_note`, link reason `grn_invoice_link`) and on its POs (the header `purchase_order_id` and its `purchase_order_bill_links` rows, `po_invoice_link`), every id fenced to the bill's company (neither column carries a foreign key, and row security admits every company a user belongs to). A document also reached through an older `document_links` row is listed once. The bill's own uploads are still linked onto its PO(s) and note at upload (`autoLinkSupplierInvoice`, `linked_entity_type = 'goods_received'`).

Decided: read-time resolution, not more link writes. Writing links would need a writer on every path that sets the bill's note or POs (create, link / unlink, draft edits) and on every path that files a PO or note document (the REST upload, `attach_entity_document`, `finalize_uploads`), plus a backfill for documents already filed, and each writer is one more place to forget; the read is one query in one function, needs no backfill, and follows an unlink or a deactivated document by itself. `autoLinkSupplierInvoice` no longer writes the PO / note documents onto the bill (the read supersedes it); the older rows it wrote are still read and are order paperwork to the "has a document" flag either way (`documentPresenceSql`).

`goods_received` becomes an entity-store kind: it is added to `AccountingEntityType`, to the ownership check, to `ENTITY_TARGET_KINDS` (create_upload_urls / finalize_uploads), and to `attach_entity_document`, with the default document type `delivery_note`. If that type is not in `VALID_DOCUMENT_TYPES`, it is added there. `find_by_external_ref` gains the kind `goods_received`.

## Known limits

- **Supplier merge moves bills, not their notes' POs.** The two merge paths (`core/duplicateMergeService.ts` and `pages/api/accounting/supplier-merge.ts`) re-parent `supplier_invoices.supplier_id` and the legacy `supplier_purchase_orders`, but not `purchase_orders.supplier_id`. A merged bill that carries a goods received note is then on the primary supplier while its note's PO stays on the duplicate, so the note no longer agrees with the bill. Nothing refuses it and nothing reports it. The merge code predates goods received notes, and a fix needs a decision on whether a merge moves the duplicate's purchase orders too. Tracked as #1708.
- **A soft-deleted legacy bill whose note was hard-deleted cannot be restored.** `supplier_invoices.grn_id` has no foreign key, and before 1021 app_user could delete notes. Undo refuses a `grn_id` that resolves to no note of the bill's company, so the bill must be entered again.
- **A third decimal on a 13-digit quantity rounds silently.** Quantities are parsed as JavaScript numbers. At the storage maximum (9999999999999.99) a double cannot hold a third decimal, so `9999999999999.991` reads as `.99` and is stored without a refusal. Smaller quantities with a third decimal are refused.

## Acceptance tests (integration, app_user role)

1. On a PO for 10, receiving 6 makes it `partially_received`; receiving 4 more makes it `received`; GRN numbers go up by one.
2. Receiving 1 more after that is refused with `over_receipt`, and nothing is written.
3. Cancelling the second GRN restores `quantity_received` to 6 and the status to `partially_received`. Cancelling the first restores 0 and `approved`.
4. Cancel is refused for a GRN on a bill, for an already-cancelled GRN, and for a GRN on a cancelled PO; a GRN on a closed PO cancels and leaves the PO closed (`mcp-goods-received-closed`). A cancelled or deleted bill frees the note.
5. A GRN PDF attaches through create_upload_urls → finalize_uploads, and get_goods_received lists it.
6. Another company's PO or GRN is refused as `not_found`. Lists never show another company's notes.
7. A repeated `external_ref` is refused and names the first GRN. After that GRN is cancelled, the ref can be used again.
8. dry_run writes nothing, and a live call with the same key then succeeds.
9. The GL is unchanged by create and by cancel.
10. Racing two creates for the same remaining quantity: one succeeds and the other is refused as `over_receipt`.
11. A bill create refuses a cancelled, foreign, unknown or malformed note, a note on another supplier's PO and a note on a PO other than the ones the bill names (header or lines), and the undo refuses the same (`mcp-goods-received-bill-supplier`); the undo of a deleted bill refuses a note cancelled meanwhile; a bill create and a cancel of its note, interleaved both ways and raced, end in one outcome (`mcp-goods-received-bills`).
12. As app_user a forged cancel UPDATE, an item edit and DELETEs of notes or items are refused; no item goes onto another company's note, or onto an existing one; receipts that disagree with their PO line are a typed refusal (`mcp-goods-received-db`).
13. After every note is cancelled, the PO can be cancelled and its header edited (`mcp-goods-received-po-loop`).
14. A note with an item of a line on another PO is refused `items_not_on_po` by the tool and the function, before any write; the cancel takes no number lock 620 and bounds its row-lock waits; a create racing a cancel never leaves two live notes with one reference (`mcp-goods-received-cancel-locks`).
15. A bill lists its note's and POs' documents whether they were filed before or after it, once, and never another company's; a legacy `goods_receipt_note` document of this company's note is visible to app_user and no other (`mcp-goods-received-docs-bill`).
16. A draft edit that changes the supplier re-judges the bill's note (refused, or with `grn_id` null / a note that agrees accepted; a cancel racing the edit is refused), and the link tools refuse leaving a bill's note on a PO it does not name (`mcp-goods-received-bill-edit`, `mcp-goods-received-po-links`). `get_goods_received` pages the widest note under the read limit, lists legacy documents and keeps an item of another PO's line (`mcp-goods-received-read`).
