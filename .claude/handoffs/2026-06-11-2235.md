## Status
The withAuth flush-before-COMMIT race is **fixed, merged, deployed, and live-verified in prod**. `withAuth` (`src/lib/auth/withAuth.ts`) previously ran the route handler — which flushes the HTTP response — INSIDE the open pg transaction and COMMITed afterward, so a client refetching immediately after a mutation could miss the write (reproduced twice in WS-C/WS-D). The fix defers the final response flush until after COMMIT/ROLLBACK. PR #169 merged to master (`99562f64`), deployed to prod at 22:29 SAST, service active with 0 restarts, journal clean. The two per-page settle/retry workarounds (payroll parallel-run + bank/queries) were removed as proof.
