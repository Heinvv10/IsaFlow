Interest cover
Operating profit divided by net interest expense. This pack
captures no interest expense line, so it is reported as not
meaningful.

Gross margin
Gross profit (revenue less cost of sales), as a percentage of
revenue.

Debtor days
Trade receivables over revenue, times the days in the
window — the reporting period on the scorecard and
highlights, one month on the trends page. That is why the
two differ.

Operating margin
Operating profit as a percentage of revenue.
Net profit margin
Net profit, as a percentage of revenue.

Creditor days
Trade payables over cost of sales, times the days in the
period. Not reported where there is no cost of sales.

EBITDA
Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation
— a non-IFRS measure, taken from operating profit (already
stated before interest and tax) plus the depreciation and
amortisation charged in the period. The bridge below is the
whole of it; there are no other adjustments.
