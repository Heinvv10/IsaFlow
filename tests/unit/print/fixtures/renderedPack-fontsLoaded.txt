Debtor days
Trade receivables over revenue, times 365 — an annualised
figure on the scorecard and highlights, whatever the length
of the reporting period. The trends page instead multiplies
each month's ratio by the days in that month, so the two do
not agree.
Creditor days
Trade payables over cost of sales, times 365 — annualised,
whatever the length of the reporting period. Not reported
where there is no cost of sales.
Cash conversion cycle
Debtor days plus inventory days, less creditor days — each
annualised on 365 days, as above. Not reported where no
account is classified as inventory.
Operating cash flow
Cash generated by operations before investing and financing
activities.
