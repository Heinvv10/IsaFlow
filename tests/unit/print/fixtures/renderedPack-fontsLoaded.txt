How this page is prepared

Commentary This page carries the board's own commentary on the period. It prints only once an approver has
signed it; until then the page says so rather than printing unapproved text.

Action panel The panel headed 'For the board' is derived from the same checks the matters page tabulates —
aged debtor concentration, unreconciled bank items, unusual journals, overdue statutory filings and months
whose expenses are a net credit — in the order the matters page prints them: matters with no single rand amount
first, then the largest by exposure. It is machine-written and states the decision each matter is waiting on, never a
recommendation.

What debtor days is measured on

The base Debtor days on the highlights and scorecard pages is taken on trade receivables of 59, the tradereceivable control account in the general ledger and the figure the balance sheet states. The open customer
subledger for the same date is 10 724, in R'000. The two are not reconciled — the matters page sets out what the
difference consists of — and this pack does not recompute the ratio on the other base. Read the day count as a
measure of the control account, not of what customers owe.

Panels that cover part of the window
Why some panels are shorter Gross margin and Debtor days are drawn over fewer than the 12 months the trends
page covers. A ratio is not measured in a month whose base is nil, so those panels start at the first month they can
be measured rather than being plotted as a zero, and each panel's own title states how many periods it covers. A
panel is omitted where its figure is meaningful in no period of the window, and the trends page says so where that
happens.

Working capital
Current assets less current liabilities, in rand.
Current ratio

Debtor days
Trade receivables over revenue, times 365 — an annualised
figure on the scorecard and highlights, whatever the length
of the reporting period. The trends page instead multiplies
each month's ratio by the days in that month, so the two
need not agree.
Creditor days
Trade payables over cost of sales, times 365 — annualised,
whatever the length of the reporting period. Not reported
where there is no cost of sales.
Cash conversion cycle
Debtor days plus inventory days, less creditor days — each
annualised on 365 days, as above. Not reported where no
account is classified as inventory.
Operating cash flow
Cash generated by operations before investing and financing
activities.

figures cannot support is not meaningful. Neither counts as
a pass or a miss.
