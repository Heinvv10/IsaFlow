## Recent changes

- 2026-11-11 (wave 5 DR): `directive_type` also takes `fixed_percentage` (IRP3(b): `tax_rate` required, a fraction; no `tax_amount`) and `fixed_amount` (IRP3(c): `tax_amount` required, the PAYE a month; no `tax_rate`). `amount` must be absent or 0 (stored 0); `effective_date` and `expiry_date` real dates, expiry on or after effective (`taxDirectiveRegularPayBody`). Create and approve refuse an overlap with another of the employee's regular-pay directives (pending or approved at create, approved at approve) with **400**, judged under an advisory lock before any write (`taxDirectiveOverlap`). `POST /[id]/apply` on one is **400** (`TaxDirectiveNotLumpSumError`): each run applies it. `DELETE` is **409** once an approved or paid run was taxed by it.
- 2026-11-11 (wave 5 DR round 1): every type: `directive_number` ≤ 50, `sars_reference` ≤ 100, dates real and 2000-2100 ("" is 400), `tax_rate` at most 4 decimals, a lump-sum `amount` below 1e13; `directive_number` and `sars_reference` masked in the request log. Regular types: rate / amount above 0, `directive_number` required (AN15, not zeros). Approve (any type) and regular create / change are **403** under Login-as. `PATCH { expiry_date }` on a regular directive sets or shortens it (400 when later, before its effective date, or before a run it taxed). Cancelling a regular directive an approved/paid run used is **409** ("set an expiry date instead"). A window over an approved period is 400.
- 2026-11-11 (wave 5 DR round 2): approving a directive (any type) as its creator is **403** while another owner/admin/manager could approve; a one-person company may, audited `sole_approver`. `DELETE` of a regular directive refuses Login-as (403). A regular directive for an independent contractor is 400 at create and approve. A run reopened after approval still counts as taxed by its directive (409 / 400 as before).
