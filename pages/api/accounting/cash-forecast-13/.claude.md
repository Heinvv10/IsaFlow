# cash-forecast-13/ (13-week cash forecast: stored inputs)

The user-entered inputs of the 13-week cash forecast (migration 999i). Services live in
`src/modules/accounting/services/cashForecast13/` (`itemsRepo`, `overridesRepo`, `payDaySetting`,
validation `storeSchemas`, refusals `storeErrors`); `storeTypes.ts` is the STORED shape (rand, dates, direction).
`engineAdapter.ts` maps it to the engine's inputs (cents, week indexes); its engine types are copies until PR 1
merges, then switch them to imports of `types.ts` / `payDates.ts`.

## Endpoints
- `GET|POST|PUT|DELETE /api/accounting/cash-forecast-13/items` — expected receipts/payments (`cash_forecast_items`). POST `{ direction, amount, date, repeat?, repeatUntil?, description, category?, accountId? }` (201; `category` from the engine's CashCategory list, default `other`); PUT `{ id, …same }` replaces every field (an omitted `accountId` clears it); DELETE `?id=`. 404 for another company's id; 400 for an account of another company, inactive, or a header (another account's parent, the budgets' `isHeaderSql`). DELETE also deletes the item's `manual:<id>` overrides, each audited, in the same tx. At most 500 items per company (409).
- `GET|PUT|DELETE /api/accounting/cash-forecast-13/overrides` — per line, per week (`cash_forecast_overrides`). PUT UPSERTS on `(lineKey, weekStart)` and answers `{ override, created }`: a duplicate is never 409. GET lists in application order, the last edit last (`updated_at, id`). At most 2000 overrides per company (409 on a new cell; re-saving an existing cell is not capped). DELETE `?id=`.
- `GET|PUT /api/accounting/cash-forecast-13/pay-day` — salary pay day on `payroll_company_settings`: `{ kind: 'day_of_month', day: 1-31 } | { kind: 'last_working_day' }`; not chosen (no row, or both columns NULL) = day 25 with `isDefault: true`.

## Rules
- Reads: any company member (cookie session). Writes: `withCsrf(withWriteAccess(...))`, owner/admin/manager.
- Every write service calls `assertNotImpersonating()` first (Login-as = 403 IMPERSONATION_FORBIDDEN, nothing written) and writes ONE audit row in the request tx (`logAuditOrThrow`; entity types `cash_forecast_item`, `cash_forecast_override`, `payroll_company_settings`) with old/new values.
- Validation failures are 400 (first issue's message, `fieldErrors` in details). Dates must be real calendar dates in 2000-01-01..2100-12-31 (999i CHECKs too); weeks are Mondays.
- A save that changes nothing writes nothing (no UPDATE, no audit row) and answers the current row.
- Caps are counted under a per-company `pg_advisory_xact_lock` in the write tx, so two concurrent creates at the cap cannot both pass.
- Overrides: `set`/`add` amounts are SIGNED in the line's own sign (a payment cell is negative; the UI sends it): `set` required, `add` required and != 0. `move_to` amount is a magnitude > 0, or null = the whole cell, with a `targetWeekStart` (Monday, not `weekStart`); only `move_to` takes a target.
- Adapter: amountCents = round(amount x 100), item payments negative; weekIndex from the Monday of today's week, weeks before it or past week 13 dropped (a move_to target outside is passed on for the engine to warn); overrides kept in the repo's order (never re-sorted on millisecond strings). A move_to amount reaches the engine positive: the engine applies it in the cell's sign.
- Items: amount > 0 (direction gives the sign); `repeatUntil` only on a repeating item, on or after `date`; description 1-200 after trim.
- Pay day: a day past the month's length = its last day (engine rule). `last_working_day` keeps the stored day so switching back restores it. `updated_by`/`updated_at` on that table are stamped by 999i's trigger (app_user cannot write them). The pay-day write moves to the payroll settings UI once that exists; this route is the interim writer.
- The DB repeats every rule (999i CHECKs, company-matched account trigger, forced RLS, trigger stamps from `auth.uid()`, per-column grants). Never write `id`, `company_id` on update, or a stamp from code.
