## Urgent: tell us straight away if
- you can see another company's name, figures or documents that aren't yours,
- money has been posted wrongly or seems to have disappeared,
- you or your team can't log in, or someone has access they shouldn't.

Stop working on that screen and contact us at https://isaflow.co.za/contact, with **URGENT** at the start of your message. Tell us the page and what you saw. Please don't forward or screenshot the other company's details more than you need to.

## Before you file a return
If a VAT201 or EMP201 figure doesn't look right, **don't file it**. Check the pre-filing exceptions, reconcile the bank, then contact us if it still looks off.

## Quick checks first
- **On a phone?** Some screens don't display properly on phones yet. Try a laptop or desktop.
- **"...is a header with sub-accounts"?** Post to one of the sub-accounts instead.
- **Form won't save?** Check dates are typed in full as DD/MM/YYYY.
- **Migration file rejected?** Make sure it's a CSV, not Excel, and check the headings against the template.
- **Recurring invoice edited?** Re-check its End Date and VAT rate afterwards.

## How to report a bug
Send us:
1. **What you were trying to do**, and the page (copy the part of the web address after `app.isaflow.co.za`).
2. **Steps** to make it happen again.
3. **What you expected**, and **what actually happened**, with the exact error message.
4. **A screenshot**, with ID numbers, tax numbers, bank details, salaries and email addresses blurred or cropped out.
5. **Your device and browser** (for example Chrome on Windows, Safari on iPhone).
6. **When** it happened, and whether it happens every time.

**Please never send** passwords, one-time PINs, ID numbers, bank account numbers, payslips or Sage login details. If we need something sensitive, we'll arrange a safe way to collect it.
