*General product help, not tax advice.*

ISAFlow **prepares** your EMP201, EMP501, IRP5/IT3(a) and VAT201 and gives you the figures and export files to file on SARS eFiling (or e@syFile Employer). **ISAFlow doesn't file with SARS for you.**

## VAT201
1. Reconcile your bank accounts for the period first.
2. Open **Accounting, SARS, VAT201** and choose the period.
3. Review the **pre-filing exceptions** and clear them. These are things to fix before you file.
4. Check the figures. The return is laid out by SARS field number, so you can compare it with the eFiling form line by line, and it ties back to your general ledger.
5. Save the draft and export the CSV if you want a copy.
6. **File and pay on eFiling** yourself.
7. Back in ISAFlow, choose **Record as filed on eFiling** (on older screens, **Mark as Submitted to SARS**) and enter the SARS reference. This only records the filing in ISAFlow; nothing is sent to SARS. If there are unresolved pre-filing exceptions, an owner or admin has to give a reason to continue.
8. To stop further changes to the period, lock it under **Accounting, SARS, VAT Period Locks**. Recording the filing doesn't lock the period on its own. Owners, admins and managers can lock a period. Only an owner or admin can unlock it, and they must give a reason, which is recorded.

## EMP201 (monthly)
1. Approve the month's pay runs.
2. Open **Accounting, SARS, EMP201** and choose the month. It shows PAYE, UIF, SDL and the ETI offset.
3. Save the draft and check it against your payroll reports.
4. **File on eFiling and pay using your own PRN** (payment reference number). SARS needs the declaration before the payment.
5. Back in ISAFlow, record the filing with the SARS reference, as for the VAT201.

## EMP501 and IRP5/IT3(a)
1. Open **Payroll, EMP501** for the tax year. Your EMP201s, payroll and payments to SARS should agree. **Export e@syFile CSV** gives you a per-employee summary for the year.
2. Open **Payroll, IRP5** to check certificate readiness. Fields that are missing (for example an employee's tax number) are listed with a link to where to fix them.
3. Choose **e@syFile IRP5** for the file to import into **e@syFile Employer**, or **Export CSV** for a summary.

The e@syFile IRP5 file is for the annual reconciliation. ISAFlow doesn't produce an interim (mid-year) certificate file yet.

## Good to know
- Due dates and rates: check the SARS tax calendar at https://www.sars.gov.za.
- For anything about how to treat a transaction for tax, ask your tax practitioner.
- If a figure looks wrong, don't file. Contact us first (see [Something looks wrong?](/help/something-looks-wrong)).
