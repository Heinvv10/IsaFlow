*General product help, not tax or legal advice.*

If you look after the books or payroll of several businesses, ISAFlow keeps each client as its own company, with its own ledger, payroll and VAT, and gives you one dashboard across all of them.

## 1. Create your account
1. Sign up at https://app.isaflow.co.za/register.
2. [Contact us](https://isaflow.co.za/contact) and tell us you're a bureau. The **bureau dashboard** is set up with you during onboarding, so this step matters. Until then you'll see the normal company dashboard.

## 2. Add your team
Give each staff member their own login. Never share logins. Invite people under **User Access** (choose **Invite a User**). Access is given per company, so invite each staff member to every client company they work on. Roles available: owner, admin, manager and viewer. Only an owner can make someone an owner, and each company always keeps at least one owner. Payroll can be run by owners, admins and managers.

## 3. Link your client companies
1. Make sure each client exists as a company in ISAFlow. To create one, use the company switcher and choose **Add Company**. If the client already uses ISAFlow, ask them to invite you to their company as an owner or admin.
2. Once the bureau is set up, open **Bureau, Manage Clients** (`/bureau/clients`) and link each company.

Only the bureau owner can link or unlink a client company, and only for companies where they're an owner or admin. Other members can view the list.

## 4. Bring each client across
For each client, run the migration wizard (see [Migrating a client from Sage](/help/migrating-a-client-from-sage)). Do one client end to end first, check the trial balance, then do the rest.

## 5. Use the bureau dashboard every morning
The bureau dashboard (`/bureau`) lists the work owed across your clients, most urgent first. Work through it from the top.

## Tips
- Use a standard chart of accounts across clients where you can. Reviews get faster and bank rules are easier to reuse.
- Split preparing and reviewing between two people where possible.
- ISAFlow prepares SARS returns for you to file on eFiling. Filing stays with you.
- POPIA: you process your clients' personal information for them, so make sure you have the right agreements in place. Please take advice on what you need.
