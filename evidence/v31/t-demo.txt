Negative total assets
What this means Total assets are (730). An asset side below zero is not a position a business can hold; it means
one or more asset accounts carry credit balances — commonly a bank account overdrawn beyond its recorded
facility, or a control account posted against. The balance sheet still foots, so the pack reports it as the ledger
states it; it should be traced to the accounts before this pack is relied on.
