Negative revenue
What this means Revenue is (5 535). Turnover below zero is not a period a business can trade — it means the
revenue accounts are in net debit, commonly credit notes or reversals exceeding the period's invoicing, or a
receipt posted to an income account the wrong way round. Every margin in this pack divides by revenue, so each
of them is reported as not meaningful rather than computed off a negative base. The pack reports the ledger as it
stands; the revenue accounts should be traced before it is relied on.
