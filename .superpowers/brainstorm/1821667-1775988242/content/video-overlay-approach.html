A — Always-On Overlay (Recommended)

Video plays and loops silently in the background forever. A left-heavy dark gradient sits on top — opaque on the left where text lives, semi-transparent on the right so the city and data river show through. Logo in the navbar, text and CTAs always visible. No timing issues, always readable.

B — Video First, Then Reveal

Video plays through the full night-to-dawn sequence (12 sec). Overlay lifts, text fades in dramatically as the sun rises. Very cinematic — but risky. Users who skip past or land mid-scroll won't see the CTA until the animation completes. Needs a skip button.

C — Glass Nav + Centred Text

Video loops. Frosted glass navbar (blur + dark tint) keeps logo always crisp. Text centred over the video with top and bottom gradient vignettes for readability. Most modern pattern — used by Apple, Notion, Linear.

My Recommendation: A or C

A if you want the city to be a backdrop — text dominates, always readable, no risk.
C if you want the video to feel like the hero — text floats over it, glass nav keeps logo crisp, most premium feel.
Avoid B — hiding the CTA behind a 12-second animation will hurt conversions.
