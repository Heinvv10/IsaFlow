✅ Proposed Premium Structure
🎬 Hero — video bg, glass nav, centred white text, "Your finances, finally clear."
🏢 Trust bar — "Trusted by X businesses" + 4–5 company logos, monochrome
📊 Stats — 3 big bold numbers (R value processed, businesses, reports) — dark bg
⚡ Feature pillars — 4 core capabilities only (not 40+). Icon + title + 1 sentence each
📱 3 feature deep-dives — trim from 8 to 3 most compelling (Invoicing, Reconciliation, AI)
💬 Testimonials — 1 featured quote with photo + specific number, then 2 smaller
💰 Pricing — keep 3 tiers, clean up visual weight
❓ FAQ — trim to 5 most important
🚀 Final CTA section — dark bg, bold "Ready to get started?" + single button (missing currently)
🔗 Footer

The 3 Big Changes That Make It Feel Premium

  • 40 features → 4 pillars: Premium brands don't list everything they do. They pick the 4 outcomes that matter and own them. The full feature list can live on a /features page.
  • 8 deep-dives → 3: Invoicing, Bank Reconciliation, and AI Capture are the most differentiated. Cut the rest or collapse them into one "and more" section.
  • Add a final CTA section: Currently the page ends on FAQ and footer. Premium sites always close with a bold "Ready?" moment before the footer — it captures people who scrolled all the way down.
